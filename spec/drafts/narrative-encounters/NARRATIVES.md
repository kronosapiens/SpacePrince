# Narrative encounter scenes

**Status: Earlier scene sketches, 2026-10-08; not approved encounters.**
Twelve fresh situations, each expressed through two signs, for evaluating setting, mood, relationships, and room for a response.
The prompts leave the Prince's preferred outcome open and work without an occupant or available joy.
The sign labels name the sign in the encounter house, not a separate planetary placement.

Two writers generated these scenes from the current [House](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md), [Sign](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix), and related concept references without consulting the archived combined experiment.
The framing follows [DRIVES.md](../../concept/DRIVES.md): possible motives and feelings belong to the Prince, while the player's interpretation and experience remain distinct.
The scenes are original adaptations, not historical astrological claims.
These sketches contain no candidate responses or economic offers.
Continue the authoring work in the [working guide](STYLE_GUIDE.md); this collection is material for possible revision rather than workflow guidance.
This draft does not require reviewing or adopting the archived encounters.
Possible later experiments are recorded under [Directions to revisit](#directions-to-revisit).

## I Self

### A scent for the skin

[Self](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#i--self) makes bodily presentation the circumstance: a perfumer has a proposal about how a stranger should smell.
She wants someone to wear her work and accept the story she tells about it.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs familiarity in Cancer and discovery in Sagittarius as different forms of the perfumer's pitch.

**Cancer**

A perfumer uncorks something that smells of the soap used in the Prince's childhood household and reaches for his wrist.
She calls it a scent that will make anyone seem familiar, though she has never met him before.

**Sagittarius**

A perfumer offers a scent she calls The End of the Road, made from plants gathered beyond the surrounding hills.
She reaches for the Prince's wrist while describing the distant port that supposedly smells exactly like her bottle.

The bottle is still in her hand, and bowls of bitter peel and crushed herbs stand open on the workbench.

## II Livelihood

### The split provision bag

[Livelihood](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ii--livelihood) places usable possessions at risk when their container fails.
The boatman wants his sail cleared so he can load it, and offers the equipment already beside him.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs inspection in Virgo and urgency in Aries as different circumstances of the same spill.

**Virgo**

The Prince's provision bag splits on a landing, scattering wrapped food, a cup, and a mending roll across a boatman's folded sail.
The boatman wants to load the sail onto his cart, but offers a shallow basket while the Prince checks what has become wet.

**Aries**

The Prince's provision bag splits as a boatman pulls a folded sail toward his cart, sending food parcels, a cup, and a mending roll sliding across the cloth.
The boatman stops with the sail taut and tells him to grab his things, pushing a shallow basket forward with his foot.

The torn bag still has sound corners, and the landing has a dry strip beside its mooring posts.

## III Communication

### The speaking tube

[Communication](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iii--communication) supplies neighboring households, familiar voices, and an exchange that requires someone else's answer.
The cousin wants the baker's company; the baker is attending to his shop.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs an established private habit in Taurus and an expanded neighborhood conversation in Aquarius.

**Taurus**

In his cousin's courtyard, the Prince finds her listening at the speaking tube that joins her kitchen to the baker's next door.
The baker has misunderstood her invitation to sit down as an order for more bread and keeps reciting what remains on his shelves.

**Aquarius**

The Prince's cousin has connected her kitchen speaking tube to the other homes around the courtyard so everyone can announce what they need.
She is trying to invite the baker over, but neighbors keep interrupting with requests addressed to entirely different rooms.

There is room beside the cousin on her kitchen stool, and a spare cup stands on the table.

## IV Home

### The table that becomes a bed

[Home](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iv--home) puts a household custom and the authority to alter it in the same piece of furniture.
The aunt wants to settle her guest, while the cousin occupies the table for a purpose of her own.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs an explicit negotiation in Libra and an arrangement left to gestures in Pisces.

**Libra**

In the family house, the table unfolds into the bed prepared for the Prince, but his cousin has covered it with a wooden puzzle.
She asks for another try while his aunt points out that the bed is his to open; a broad serving tray lies beside the pieces.

**Pisces**

In the family house, the table unfolds into the Prince's bed, but his cousin has fallen asleep over a wooden puzzle scattered across it.
His aunt says he may wake her and open the bed, then sets a broad serving tray beside the pieces without touching her shoulder.

A narrow bench runs beside the hearth, within sight of the table.

## V Creativity

### The bowl of moving color

[Creativity](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#v--creativity) supplies a pastime whose result need not outlast the moment.
The woman offers company and materials while retaining her own wishes about the game.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs an unnamed private image in Scorpio and an invitation to verbal invention in Gemini.

**Scorpio**

Beside a fountain, a woman floats drops of colored ink in a bowl of thickened water and draws them into shapes with a comb.
She offers the Prince the comb but asks him not to guess what her dark, closed spiral represents; a clean sheet waits beside the bowl.

**Gemini**

Beside a fountain, a woman floats colored ink in a bowl of thickened water, calling the same blot a fish, a slipper, and a magistrate in quick succession.
She offers the Prince her comb and challenges him to make something she cannot rename; a clean sheet waits beside the bowl.

A slow draft shifts the ink even when nobody touches the bowl.

## VI Labor

### Beneath the wooden moon

[Labor](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vi--labor) concerns the workers whose bodies sustain somebody else's occasion.
The stagehand needs to free the rope, the manager wants the moon held until its cue, and the actor is taking more time.
The [sign reference](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) informs an imposed sequence in Capricorn and a division between visible and concealed participation in Leo.

**Capricorn**

Beneath a small theatre, a stagehand has the Prince holding the winch that keeps a wooden moon above the actors while she untangles its lowering rope.
The stage manager insists they wait for his signal, though the winch has a holding pin and the actor overhead has begun the speech again.

**Leo**

Beneath a small theatre, a stagehand has the Prince holding the winch that keeps a wooden moon above an actor who is prolonging a speech to gather applause.
She is untangling the lowering rope beside the winch's unused holding pin, while the stage manager hisses at them to keep their work silent.

The stagehand can reach the handle beside the Prince, and voices carry through the floorboards.

## VII Relationships

### An Arm at the Door

The [Seventh](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vii--relationships) supplies a proposed partnership whose terms either person can change or refuse.
The woman wants to enter the assembly with a particular companion while retaining a say in how they are presented.
The [Gemini and Scorpio versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish playful mutual description from a shared appearance whose private history remains negotiable.

**Gemini**

At the assembly door, a woman you spoke with at supper offers her arm: she wants company for the opening dance, provided you introduce each other instead of leaving it to the herald.
She proposes announcing you as a collector of improbable stories and asks what you would call her.

**Scorpio**

At the assembly door, a woman you spoke with at supper offers her arm for the opening dance, but asks you to keep that earlier meeting between you.
The herald has been instructed to announce where each pair first met; she wants to enter with you without supplying him a story.

The invitation extends only to the opening dance.
The woman is waiting for an answer, and neither of you has yet stepped into the herald's hearing.

## VIII Transformation

### The Dancer's Hem

The [Eighth](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#viii--transformation) supplies the distribution of belongings after a death, with authority divided among the living.
The dancer's sister wants to carry out the distribution; members of the company want different pieces of a garment they once handled together.
The [Aries and Virgo versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish a separation already being urged forward from one conducted through careful examination.

**Aries**

A dancer you knew has died, leaving her immense embroidered rehearsal skirt to the people who appeared beside her; her sister has brought it to the empty dressing room for everyone to take a piece.
She offers you the shears first, while another dancer holds the bright red panel against his chest and says he wants that one.

**Virgo**

A dancer you knew has died, leaving her immense embroidered rehearsal skirt to the people who appeared beside her; her sister has spread it inside out across the empty dressing room.
The panels separate at their old seams, but the sister asks each person to choose carefully because some contain repairs made by other members of the company.

Nobody has cut the skirt yet.
The sister has brought cloth wrappings for its pieces, and the company is gathering around the table.

## IX Pilgrimage

### The River Under Stone

The [Ninth](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ix--pilgrimage) supplies a distant observance that remains under local authority while making room for a visitor.
The attendant wants the ceremony to continue; the other pilgrims bring their own forms of remembrance to a river they cannot see.
The [Pisces and Libra versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish an invitation to supply an image from an invitation to find a form with another person.

**Pisces**

At a shrine far from your country, pilgrims pour water over a stone floor above a river buried by an ancient landslide.
The attendant offers you a pitcher and says some visitors name a river, some imitate its sound, and some pour without speaking; she will not tell you which river to imagine.

**Libra**

At a shrine far from your country, pilgrims pour water over a stone floor above a river buried by an ancient landslide.
The attendant pairs you with a local pilgrim, who wants to pour for the vanished river while you name a living one; she leaves you both to decide whose water touches the stone first.

Water is being poured elsewhere across the floor, and people without pitchers watch from an open passage.
The attendant is close enough to answer a practical question.

## X Achievement

### The Judge's Apron

The [Tenth](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#x--achievement) supplies temporary public authority and a verdict other people will hear and act on.
The steward wants a selection for the ceremonial table, while the bakers want their work considered on terms they believe it deserves.
The [Aquarius and Taurus versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish a disputed category from a demand for tangible evidence.

**Aquarius**

At the bakers' exhibition, the steward ties the judge's apron around you: your choice will occupy the central platter at the ceremonial supper.
A young baker has entered a dark, hollow loaf served by breaking it open, and the other entrants insist that a loaf with so little crumb should not compete beside theirs.

**Taurus**

At the bakers' exhibition, the steward ties the judge's apron around you: your choice will occupy the central platter at the ceremonial supper.
A magnificent glazed loaf stands beside a plain round one, and the plain loaf's maker asks you to taste through the center before deciding from the crust.

The platter is empty, a bread knife lies beside it, and the steward's deputy is waiting near the table.
The crowd can see the loaves but has not tasted them.

## XI Friendship

### The Great Sled

The [Eleventh](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xi--friendship) supplies practical support from people who want the Prince included in a shared pleasure.
The friends have made his participation possible before he arrives, and each still has a place in the outing rather than merely serving as his audience.
The [Leo and Capricorn versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish a conspicuous welcome from one expressed through preparation.

**Leo**

Friends have hauled an enormous sled to the top of a snowy hill and painted a crown on its front board for your arrival.
They have left you the leading seat, but the friend holding the brake declares that she intends to scream louder than anyone on the way down.

**Capricorn**

Friends have hauled an enormous sled to the top of a snowy hill and tucked a folded blanket into the middle for your arrival.
The friend at the brake is checking the downhill track, while another holds the runners steady and tells you there is nothing left to prepare before you sit down.

The friends can launch once everyone riding has settled into a place.
A packed footpath runs down beside the sled track.

## XII The Hidden

### The Visiting Shutter

The [Twelfth](../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xii--the-hidden) supplies restricted access to a particular person's life: the keeper controls the locked door, while the resident controls the visiting shutter.
The resident wants company on terms she can still set herself, and the keeper is enforcing the conditions of the visit.
The [Sagittarius and Cancer versions](../../concept/PLANET_MATRIX.md#sign-matrix) distinguish an appetite for unfamiliar experience from a carefully bounded welcome to familiar comfort.

**Sagittarius**

A traveler confined in the customs lodging while officials examine her papers opens her visiting shutter when you pass; the keeper permits conversation and food through the opening but keeps the door locked.
She asks for a description of the strangest meal you have eaten, insisting that she has already heard enough guesses about when she will be released.

**Cancer**

A traveler confined in the customs lodging while officials examine her papers opens her visiting shutter when you pass; the keeper permits conversation and food through the opening but keeps the door locked.
She asks you to sit where she can see you peel your plum, saying the sound reminds her of her sister's kitchen, but she does not want questions about her family.

There is a bench beside the shutter and a small saucer on its ledge.
The keeper has neither the papers nor the authority to end her confinement.

## Directions to revisit

Discussion recorded 2026-10-08; further exploration is deferred.
Continue generating and reviewing individual encounters through the existing process to see how far that format can go.
These notes add no generation steps or implementation requirements.

The [walking-simulator article](https://entaltostudios.com/our-top-5-walking-simulator-games-you-shouldnt-miss/) prompted several possible influences:

- [Gone Home](https://gonehome.com/): objects and their arrangement let players infer relationships and history.
- [What Remains of Edith Finch](https://www.giantsparrow.com/games/finch/): distinct actions and emotional tones give short stories individual character.
- [Firewatch](https://www.firewatchgame.com/): interpersonal choices suggest opportunities for conversation whose practical subject carries a personal concern.
- [The Stanley Parable](https://www.stanleyparable.com/): choice and expectation suggest letting the Prince question or refuse an assigned role.
- [Dear Esther](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/road-to-the-igf-thechineseroom-s-i-dear-esther-i-): partial, ambiguous accounts leave room for interpretation.

These are proposed adaptations for Space Prince.
An isolated scene can suggest a history; relationships built through shared experience need continuity.
One decision per encounter could still support a larger experience, but connected stories would expand the current scope.

A possible later experiment is several independently playable encounters sharing a household, workshop, festival, or recurring characters.
Different houses could reveal different aspects of that setting, allowing familiarity and interpretation to accumulate across encounters read in different orders.
Existing chart condition and run progress could also shape perspectives, while specific callbacks would require evidence of the earlier action.

Chained encounters remain a separate possibility with additional costs: persistent narrative state, continuation eligibility, contract validation, and consistency across routes and ended runs.
Compact flags alone would not address that complexity; transaction costs would need measurement.
Revisit these possibilities if work on individual encounters reveals a limitation worth that added scope.

# House I scene briefs

These original situations use [House I](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#i--self) for bodily presence and introduction, [Mercury I](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#mercury-i--self) for an additional response that changes how the Prince is met, and the [sign notes](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) for particular people, practices, and places.
Mercury's contribution belongs to a choice rather than the Prince's prescribed personality.
The possible exchanges below precede pricing; each finished menu also allows refusal or an available gift.
During expansion, use the participation the scene actually supports: a fitting, greeting, or gift does not need an added paid lesson or modeling job.
Many decisions can simply choose among kinds of care, self-presentation, or refusal; Mercury's changed action need not combine both ordinary benefits.

## Aries

1. `self-aries-shorn` — A shearer offers to cut away the winter hair that hides your face, but his apprentice has already heated the curling irons; spring renewal and hurried wool-workers, comic pleasure; choose plain care or an elaborate treatment, while Mercury lets you describe the cut with the apprentice's own wool samples.
2. `self-aries-first-step` — A dancing master testing the spring floor insists that a person should enter a room on their stronger foot, and asks you to prove which it is; direct beginnings, playful challenge; try a brief stance or sustained exercise, with Mercury inventing an entrance that uses both.
3. `self-aries-borrowed-name` — The woman at the bathhouse remembers you by the nickname shouted during an old race and has written it on your towel; quick familiarity, awkward welcome; accept the welcome, pay for private attention, or introduce yourself afresh, with Mercury turning her own nickname into a reciprocal introduction.
4. `self-aries-sleeves` — A smith offers to refit your stiff cuffs because you cannot raise your hands to greet her; heat and practical initiative, gift; choose lighter movement or firmer support, with Mercury showing her an unexpected fastening that permits both.
5. `self-aries-spring-water` — Two lambing assistants rinse the night from their faces at a spring and invite you into water they admit is painfully cold; new-season vigor, mischievous generosity; take the warm cloth, pay for their spare hot basin, or join the cold immersion, with Mercury arranging a quick alternating wash.

## Taurus

2. `self-taurus-voice` — A singer offers you her room's last bowl of broth after hearing your cracked greeting, but will trade a lesson for carrying the water herself; throat, pleasure, and practical exchange, care; accept nourishment or undertake vocal practice, with Mercury finding a comfortable note through conversation.
3. `self-taurus-sole` — A cobbler recognizes the old way you wear down one heel and lays out a padded sole beside a firm one; durable craft and familiar habit, quiet dilemma; choose immediate relief or stronger footing, with Mercury asking for different support under heel and toe.
4. `self-taurus-clay-print` — A potter has kept the handprint you left in clay as a child and offers to compare it with your present palm; earth and patient keeping, tenderness; receive hand care, pay for a careful strength exercise, or merely lay the hands together, with Mercury answering the old mark with a thumbprint.
5. `self-taurus-necklace` — A jeweler's heavy sample collar makes you stand straighter, but her assistant keeps offering to take its weight while she measures; possession felt on the body, mixed pleasure; model briefly or hold the pose for a larger fee, with Mercury suggesting a counterweight that makes the fitting easier.

## Gemini

4. `self-gemini-echo` — A young tutor demonstrates your own greeting in three voices, then realizes you are standing behind him; imitation and quick invention, embarrassment; let him teach a breathing trick, demand an honest demonstration, or leave the joke alone, with Mercury offering him a greeting he cannot anticipate.

## Leo

## Virgo

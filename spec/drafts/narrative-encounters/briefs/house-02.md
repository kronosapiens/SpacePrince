# House II scene briefs

These original situations follow [House II](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ii--livelihood): provisions and movable resources, including the pleasure of having enough, rather than a moral verdict on acquiring money.
The [sign notes](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply trades, seasons, and different habits of keeping or exchanging things.
Proposed exchanges concern what can be consumed, earned, or practiced here; no inventory, debt, or future delivery is required.
House II has no joy, and every menu retains a way through without a purchase or unaffordable exertion.

## Aries

1. `livelihood-aries-first-loaf` — A baker offers the first spring loaf cheaply if you will take it before it cools, while the next tray still needs carrying from the oven; impatient beginnings and heat, tempting bargain; take a modest meal, pay for fuller nourishment, or earn more through a longer carrying task.
2. `livelihood-aries-fleece` — A wool buyer will pay for the dirty first clipping by weight, but the shearer says washing it brings more and points to the steaming trough; wool trade and haste, practical dilemma; take the small commission, exert yourself for a larger return, or buy relief instead.
3. `livelihood-aries-first-wages` — An apprentice bursts out of the smithy with his first wages and tries to repay a kindness you had forgotten, though he has bought nothing for himself; first earnings and direct gratitude, awkward generosity; accept the coins, spend part on immediate care for both of you, or return them without turning the exchange into a debt.
4. `livelihood-aries-coals` — A foundry worker lets you gather the fallen charcoal for resale, warning that reaching the larger pieces means kneeling on still-hot ground; heat and direct acquisition, burden; choose a modest free return, a larger physical commitment, or leave the sweepings.
5. `livelihood-aries-new-tongs` — A smith has made spring tongs so strong that she challenges anyone to close them once, then offers the use of her bench instead of a sale; new tools and direct trial, playful practicality; pay for controlled grip practice, earn a fee through the harder demonstration, or leave the tool where it belongs.

## Taurus

3. `livelihood-taurus-two-handles` — A farmer brings back the carrying yoke you sold him years ago, saying the handles have finally worn to fit someone with your hands; familiar assets and stubborn generosity, gift; accept its sale value, use it for a strengthening exercise before returning it, or let him keep it without payment.
4. `livelihood-taurus-full-larder` — A cook must clear a crowded larder before new milk arrives and lets you choose between a rich private supper and simple portions for everyone with you; abundance held too long, generosity; concentrated or distributed recovery, with a small sale payment as another benefit.
5. `livelihood-taurus-worn-handle` — A tool seller will pay for your demonstration of a worn handle whose comfort makes his newer stock look bad; proven assets and quiet pride, amused bargain; test briefly for modest pay, undertake a stronger grip exercise, or accept his offered drink.

## Gemini

## Leo

5. `livelihood-leo-summer-ice` — A seller has brought a little mountain ice through the summer heat and insists every buyer see the cloth lifted before choosing; costly magnificence, brief pleasure; buy focused cooling, choose cheaper shared shade and water, or earn Light helping move the insulated chest.

## Virgo

3. `livelihood-virgo-leftovers` — A cook has labeled every remaining dish with how soon it must be eaten and asks you to choose before she begins crossing things out; stores and precise timing, comic plenty; select one nourishing portion, a lighter shared meal, or a modest cash payment for clearing the table.
4. `livelihood-virgo-unravel` — A cloth merchant will buy the thread from an unfinished sleeve, but unwinding it neatly takes longer than cutting it free; practical recovery of value, dilemma; accept a small return, take a careful hand-work burden for more, or pay for a lesson that improves your grip.

## Libra

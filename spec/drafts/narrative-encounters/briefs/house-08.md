# House VIII briefs

[House VIII](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#viii--transformation) supplies concrete endings, inheritances, and resources controlled by others; [SIGN_NOTES](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) varies how those affairs occur.
These are original situations, not claims that bereavement produces growth.
Payments settle here, objects are used or relinquished here, and each menu retains an available way to decline; no joy belongs to this house.

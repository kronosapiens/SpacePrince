# House V scene briefs

These original situations use [House V](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#v--creativity) for play, children, courtship, making, and pleasure that needs no useful product or public rank.
[Venus V](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#venus-v--creativity) supplies additional ways of attending to a wanted person, sensation, or creation; the [sign notes](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply varied practices and manners.
Participation may choose among pleasures, commitment sizes, payment by effort or Light, and individual or shared enjoyment without giving every gathering a paid job.
Any revival direction below welcomes someone back into the immediate occasion; the finished menu still fits five choices and permits refusal or an available gift.

## Aries

1. `creativity-aries-first-dance` — A dancer insists the spring dance should begin before the musicians finish tuning and offers you her hand while everyone else waits; eagerness and beginnings, delight with a condition; join her demanding pace, wait for gentler music, or enjoy the first cup, with Venus letting the unfinished tuning become their accompaniment.
2. `creativity-aries-clay-ram` — A child has made a ram from clay and wants to see whether its enormous horns will hold before the body dries; the emblem and impatient making, comic suspense without a wager; support the heavy figure, fund enough fresh clay to remake it freely, or let the child choose, with Venus making the sagging shape part of its charm.
3. `creativity-aries-red-thread` — Two children race to wind a red thread around a courtyard and invite you to be the turning post, though they have already changed direction twice; quick motion and wool craft, absurd play; take the strenuous role, sit and choose the next turn, or leave the game, with Venus joining the children in a slower moving pattern.
4. `creativity-aries-new-song` — A singer begins a love song before deciding whom it addresses and stops at your table when the next line requires a name; direct desire and invention, flirtation; offer a name, buy another verse for the table, or listen without entering it, with Venus answering her line in the tune she has just invented.
5. `creativity-aries-sparks` — A smith has saved bright harmless filings for a little spinning display and insists it will look better if you help turn the handle faster; heat craft and immediate effect, exhilaration; choose a short turn or sustained physical effort, or pay to sit and watch, with Venus slowing it until individual lights can be followed.

## Virgo

# House VI scene briefs

These original situations use [House VI](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vi--labor) for necessary work, illness, receiving care, small animals, and limited control over the conditions of effort.
[Mars VI](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#mars-vi--labor) informs an added choice that changes a method, tool, pace, or boundary and therefore contains a bodily burden.
The [sign notes](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply the work, season, and people's manner; they do not determine the Prince's temperament.
Economic directions may trade effort for immediate pay or gains, buy care, share a burden, or refuse it; no shift creates a continuing obligation.
Every finished menu has a way through at zero Light, including where declining costs an ordinary Light loss.

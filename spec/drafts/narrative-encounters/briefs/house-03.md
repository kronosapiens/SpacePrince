# House III scene briefs

These original situations use [House III](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iii--communication) for siblings, neighbors, familiar routes, and ordinary messages whose reception matters now.
[Moon III](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#moon-iii--communication) supplies additional approaches through shared habits, remembered details, and practical reassurance; [sign references](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) shape the people and the exchange.
Economic directions remain provisional and should follow the chosen participation, without adding a transaction merely to fill a menu.
In particular, answering a relative or receiving a welcome does not need an unrelated delivery fee or a new labor task.
Each finished scene also offers an available gift or refusal at zero Light.

2. `communication-aries-hot-pan` — A neighbor brings back your pan while it is still hot because she cannot wait to share what she cooked in it; heat and direct generosity, comic care; share the meal or take the portion she offers for the road, with Moon setting both portions down so everyone can sit.
3. `communication-aries-first-word` — Your younger sister wants to deliver her own complaint to the shoemaker next door but keeps asking you to begin it for her; directness and interrupted initiative, small dilemma; speak the first sentence, stand beside her while she begins, or leave her the conversation, with Moon using the signal you used when she wanted you nearby.
4. `communication-aries-hailstones` — The familiar carrier reaches your door through spring hail, insisting the letter stayed dry even though his sleeves did not; equinoctial weather and eagerness, mutual care; share warming food, pay for his offered return errand, or carry the remaining bundle through the short storm for his fee, with Moon moving the exchange beside the stove.
5. `communication-aries-shortcut` — A neighbor has opened a direct path across his newly dug garden and insists you try it, despite the deep mud between you and the tea he has prepared; new ground and cheerful certainty, playful inconvenience; brave the mud for the welcome, ask him to bring the tray to the gate, or greet him from the lane, with Moon tracing the old stepping places together.

# House IX briefs

[House IX](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ix--pilgrimage) gives each encounter a foreign practice, subject of study, or observance whose authority belongs to someone present.
[Sign references](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) shape the practice and its people; the [Sun in IX](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#sun-ix--pilgrimage) supplies an additional way to participate with an understood conviction.
The Sun directions below are authored actions, not new lore or predictions; ordinary help and a way to decline remain available without them.

## Aries

1. `pilgrimage-aries-fire-school`: A foreign smith teaches novices to judge iron by its color and catches you watching the sparks instead; firecraft and direct instruction, curiosity; buy a short lesson or work the bellows for it; Sun: join the smith in drawing the bright iron from the fire.
2. `pilgrimage-aries-first-step`: Pilgrims at a spring shrine argue about who may take the year's first step onto the thawed path; beginnings and eagerness, comedy; pay for warm lodging or join the strenuous ascent; Sun: begin the common song while everyone steps together.
3. `pilgrimage-aries-red-ink`: A teacher writes every disputed word in red and has exhausted her red ink before reaching the passage you came to study; sharp controversy, dry humor; fund more ink or copy the plain passage for pay; Sun: state which line you can affirm and discuss it closely.
4. `pilgrimage-aries-bare-hearth`: A household abroad welcomes guests by leaving its hearth cold until the guest chooses the first wood; fire and decisive hospitality, gift; choose warming rest or an exacting lesson in kindling; Sun: light the hearth with the hosts rather than commanding them.
5. `pilgrimage-aries-running-prayer`: A young guide explains that the sanctuary's prayer is spoken while running and offers to walk it with you instead; movement and fresh initiative, pleasure; take gentle instruction or a strenuous full circuit; Sun: take up the refrain while the guide carries the running verses.

## Taurus

1. `pilgrimage-taurus-bread-school`: A baker abroad teaches by touch and keeps asking why you need a name for the dough's readiness; tactile craft and patient habit, curiosity; pay for the lesson or accept warm bread; Sun: show her when your own dough is ready.
2. `pilgrimage-taurus-bell-note`: A monastery's singer lets a low note sound until visitors stop trying to improve it; steady voice and bodily pleasure, relief; rest while listening or join sustained vocal practice; Sun: hold the answering note until the whole chamber rings.
3. `pilgrimage-taurus-sacred-ox`: Pilgrims must wait for a sacred ox to finish eating before crossing, and its keeper has brought another armful of hay; animal custom and unhurried plenty, comedy; buy the waiting meal or take the strenuous upper path; Sun: ask to join the keeper in feeding it.
4. `pilgrimage-taurus-clay-map`: A teacher models the valley in clay so visitors can follow water with their fingers, but a proud pupil keeps smoothing the flood channels; useful earthcraft, disagreement; pay for a private lesson or help remake the model; Sun: trace the water's whole route for the class.
5. `pilgrimage-taurus-guest-loaf`: A foreign village gives every pilgrim a loaf and forbids thanking the donor until after eating; nourishment and settled custom, awkward generosity; eat for deep relief, share it for broader relief, or decline; Sun: join the thanks in the words you understand.

## Gemini

3. `pilgrimage-gemini-double-chant`: A shrine chants the same verse in two languages at once, and a visitor asks which one the god understands; paired voices and mediation, curiosity; listen at rest or learn one demanding part; Sun: sing a verse whose meaning you can explain.
4. `pilgrimage-gemini-margin`: A foreign student's marginal notes argue with every line of a revered text, including the blank dedication; expansive curiosity, dry humor; purchase a focused reading or help copy the disputation for pay; Sun: defend one claim aloud rather than inheriting either voice.
5. `pilgrimage-gemini-crossroads-sign`: A road school teaches navigation beside a signpost deliberately pointing the wrong way, to your guide's embarrassment; roads and testing accounts, mild indignation; take a practical lesson or accept shelter; Sun: turn the lesson toward finding the true direction together.

## Cancer

3. `pilgrimage-cancer-bowl`: Visitors to a river temple carry water in bowls inherited from their mothers, and the keeper offers you an unclaimed one; vessels and belonging, gentle dilemma; carry the full observance or rest at the public spring; Sun: use the offered bowl without inventing an ancestry for it.

2. `pilgrimage-leo-lion-teacher`: A hunter teaching animal tracks refuses to tell his famous lion story until someone notices the small print beside the large one; pride and attention, comedy; buy the full lesson or help trace the rocky route; Sun: describe the evidence before asking for the tale.

5. `pilgrimage-leo-generous-host`: A host announces that your distant homeland must have a finer feast than hers and waits for you to contradict her; magnanimity and pride, affectionate comedy; enjoy the feast or contribute its ingredients; Sun: ask her to show everyone how to share the great central dish.

## Virgo

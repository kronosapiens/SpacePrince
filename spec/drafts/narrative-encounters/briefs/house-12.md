# House XII briefs

[House XII](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xii--the-hidden) concerns lives outside ordinary access, including chosen solitude as well as imposed confinement.
[Sign references](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) make those lives particular; [Saturn in XII](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#saturn-xii--the-hidden) adds a workable limit or method within the circumstances.
No visit cures an exile or creates a later obligation, and no choice must purchase its way out; participation and its costs end in the scene.

## Aries

1. `hidden-aries-charcoal`: A charcoal burner living beyond the last houses offers a fee to tend the kiln while he sleeps but forbids anyone to enter his hut; firecraft and guarded independence, burden; take a short or long hot watch; Saturn: mark one safe circuit and tend only those vents.
2. `hidden-aries-new-hermit`: A new hermit has cleared a retreat so vigorously that he now has nowhere shaded to sit and asks you not to call it a mistake; beginnings and exposed haste, comedy; buy his spare mat or help lift a screen for pay; Saturn: set the screen at the one usable shaded corner.
3. `hidden-aries-cell-exercise`: A confined runner has worn a track into his cell and asks you to count laps through the door, paying for as long as your voice lasts; movement within limits, restless pleasure; count a brief or exhausting session; Saturn: set a finite interval and count it once.
4. `hidden-aries-red-door`: A woman hiding from a hostile household has painted her borrowed door bright red because she is tired of pretending nobody lives there; visibility held at a boundary, defiance; deliver paid supplies through the side passage or leave them at the threshold; Saturn: agree on one screened delivery point.
5. `hidden-aries-unlit-forge`: A smith who chose to close his shop invites you into the cold forge and asks you not to suggest lighting it; fire deliberately withheld, relief; purchase private rest or accept a brief quiet seat; Saturn: shut the street-facing shutters and let the room stay cold.

## Taurus

1. `hidden-taurus-locked-pasture`: An excluded herdsman rents the field outside the village and asks you to drive his cattle past people who will not speak to him; fixed belonging and animals, burden; take the short paid drive or the longer private route; Saturn: use the gate that avoids the crowded lane.
2. `hidden-taurus-orchard-alone`: A woman who rents an orchard for solitude has put cherries outside the gate so callers will stop coming inside to thank her; bodily pleasure and firm privacy, dry humor; accept a small restorative gift or pay for the secluded bench; Saturn: eat beneath the outer tree and leave the gate closed.
3. `hidden-taurus-muffled-song`: A singer rehearses behind thick curtains because she wants one song that no audience has claimed; voice and guarded pleasure, intimacy; buy a quiet listening hour or join a difficult low part; Saturn: agree on one verse and let the rest remain hers.
4. `hidden-taurus-heavy-door`: A retreat's oak door keeps its residents private but has swollen shut, and the keeper will pay to open it without breaking its panel; fixed material and chosen enclosure, practical burden; force it for full pay or buy help; Saturn: work the hinge pins loose first.
5. `hidden-taurus-hidden-supper`: Someone barred from a family celebration has ordered the same supper in a room next door and wants company that will not report back; appetite and excluded belonging, tenderness; purchase deep shared quiet or accept the small free portion; Saturn: close the connecting shutter before sitting.

## Gemini

1. `hidden-gemini-opposite-windows`: Two prisoners can see each other's windows but cannot hear, and each pays you to repeat the other's increasingly elaborate joke; divided voices and communication, comedy; carry a short or long exchange; Saturn: agree to deliver one complete joke from each.
2. `hidden-gemini-blank-pages`: A writer has rented an attic to finish nothing for a day and asks you to stop admiring the blank pages; words withheld and chosen escape, relief; pay for a private desk or share silent rest; Saturn: turn the pages face down and leave the desk unused.
3. `hidden-gemini-unposted-letter`: An exile has written three versions of a letter he cannot send and wants someone to read them aloud without choosing the best; multiple voices and blocked roads, sorrow; take a paid reading or buy a quiet interval beside him; Saturn: read the version he chooses himself.
4. `hidden-gemini-hidden-actor`: An actor resting from the stage answers every knock in a different voice, then asks you to tell the innkeeper he is alone; masks and guarded identity, comedy; pay for adjoining quiet or carry his paid errand outside; Saturn: agree on one plain message and leave the voices to him.
5. `hidden-gemini-two-keys`: A secluded scholar passes you indistinguishable keys to the gate and her closed study, then leaves to fetch a spare; access and confusing signs, privacy dilemma; use the private study key, rent the public lodge’s reading seat, or return both; Saturn: mark the gate key and return the other untouched.

## Cancer

1. `hidden-cancer-seawall`: A banished sailor sits where he can see his home's kitchen smoke and pays for an account of what the harbor sounds like today; sea memory and separation, tenderness; tell a brief account or stay through a cold paid evening; Saturn: describe today's harbor once and share the lee of the wall.
2. `hidden-cancer-curtained-window`: A woman keeps her room curtained after an absence and asks you to bring the sound of the rain closer without letting neighbors see in; protected feeling and water, relief; buy deep private rest beside her or carry the heavy screen; Saturn: open only the sheltered upper pane.
3. `hidden-cancer-locked-cupboard`: A retired host has locked away the cups people still expect her to fill and offers you the plain one she uses alone; hospitality withdrawn, quiet pleasure; accept a modest drink or pay for private lodging; Saturn: wash your own cup and leave the cupboard shut.
4. `hidden-cancer-island-room`: A sanctuary's tide-cut room is available until the causeway floods, and its keeper insists you need no reason to want it; water and bounded retreat, gift; take brief rest or pay for deeper shelter; Saturn: choose the full quiet interval and leave at the marked tide.
5. `hidden-cancer-mother-silence`: A person excluded by her kin keeps their old letters face down and asks you to sort only the envelopes; memory under protection, delicate work; take a modest paid sorting or a longer tiring task; Saturn: bundle them by date without opening the letters.

## Leo

1. `hidden-leo-back-curtain`: A former singer listens to her successor from behind the back curtain and cannot decide how much of the familiar song to hear; recognition deliberately concealed, unsettled pleasure; listen privately or join the audience together; Saturn: agree to hear a whole verse and then withdraw to the quiet dressing room.
2. `hidden-leo-unused-throne`: An excluded courtier stores his ceremonial chair in a shed and offers you a seat on the plain crate beside it; grandeur without access, bitter humor; accept quiet recovery or help move the heavy chair for pay; Saturn: cover the chair and leave it where it stands.
3. `hidden-leo-hot-gallery`: A gallery closed to the public has become unbearably hot, but its keeper wants one afternoon alone with the pictures; fixed summer and private admiration, practical dilemma; pay for the shaded room or haul the large shutters for wages; Saturn: open the high vent while keeping the public door locked.
4. `hidden-leo-lion-keeper`: A keeper living apart with an old tame lion asks you to replace the straw while he leads it behind the inner gate; large animals and guarded authority, trust with limits; take the tiring paid task or fund another pair of hands; Saturn: clear one safe bay after securing the partition.
5. `hidden-leo-no-birthday`: A woman who normally hosts extravagant birthdays asks the inn to announce none of this one and has bought a single perfect peach; pride and chosen privacy, gift; share the restorative peach or leave her the whole quiet room; Saturn: turn away the well-meaning visitor at the agreed hour.

## Virgo

1. `hidden-virgo-unsent-corrections`: An isolated copyist keeps correcting notices he is no longer allowed to post and pays you to read them through the shutter; exactness without access, sorrow; read a short selection or the tiring whole stack; Saturn: ask him to choose the one correction he most wants heard.
2. `hidden-virgo-clean-boundary`: A quarantine keeper has cleaned the visitor's bench so thoroughly that its wet surface prevents anyone sitting on it; careful care and restricted contact, comedy; pay for a dry resting place or help carry replacement boards; Saturn: cover one dry section and sit at the required distance.
3. `hidden-virgo-hidden-herbs`: A herbalist on retreat has left labeled remedies outside her door but a note asking that nobody bring her a new patient today; useful skill and withdrawn availability, relief; purchase focused care or accept the small prepared gift; Saturn: use what is already set out and leave the door quiet.
4. `hidden-virgo-counted-steps`: A confined bookkeeper counts the steps between walls and asks you to check his arithmetic rather than ask how he feels; precise private agency, tenderness; count his actual steps through the grating for a small fee or listen without taking the task; Saturn: measure once with a knotted cord and leave him the result.
5. `hidden-virgo-unfinished-list`: A caretaker rents a little room where nobody may assign work, but she has brought a list and keeps reaching for it; habitual service and chosen solitude, gentle humor; purchase deep rest or share a brief pause; Saturn: put the list outside with the boots until the visit ends.

## Libra

1. `hidden-libra-two-invitations`: An exile receives invitations from both sides of a dispute that still forbids him entering the town; ceremonial courtesy and real exclusion, anger; read the letters for pay or buy sheltered company outside; Saturn: ask which invitation he wants answered in his own words now.
2. `hidden-libra-equal-curtains`: A retreat assigns identical curtains to unequal windows, leaving one visitor plainly visible from the street; formal balance and actual privacy, dry humor; pay for the private alcove or move heavy screens for wages; Saturn: place the available cloth where the sightline crosses.
3. `hidden-libra-no-company`: A hospitable couple has posted a polite request for no visitors, and each privately fears the other will feel rude if nobody is welcomed; courtesy within chosen enclosure, comedy; accept the outer bench's rest or pay for a separate room; Saturn: take their written request seriously and close the gate.
4. `hidden-libra-quiet-witness`: A person waiting outside a closed hearing asks you to witness only that he came, without claiming to know his case; public exclusion and carefully bounded fairness, tension; take a paid cold wait or fund a sheltered seat; Saturn: witness the arrival once and agree on a departure time.
5. `hidden-libra-unread-review`: A secluded artist has hidden a cruel review beneath a favorable one and asks you to read neither while you sit; judgment withheld and protected company, relief; choose paid private rest or modest shared quiet; Saturn: move both papers from the table and let the visit stay ordinary.

## Scorpio

1. `hidden-scorpio-cellar-light`: A woman concealing herself in a cellar wants the lamp lit but fears its line beneath the door; darkness and defended access, practical tension; pay for warm shelter or help haul a screen into place; Saturn: cover the crack and light one small sheltered lamp.
2. `hidden-scorpio-accusation`: A confined man has copied the accusation against him onto every scrap of paper and asks you to read until he tells you to stop; persistence inside enclosure, burden; take a short or long paid reading; Saturn: agree to read one complete copy and then sit in silence.
3. `hidden-scorpio-walled-garden`: A gardener has closed the gate on everyone who offered to improve the place and invites you in only if you will leave the wild corner alone; guarded agency and untended growth, pleasure; pay for quiet rest or do limited paid weeding; Saturn: mark the agreed bed and work only there.
4. `hidden-scorpio-hidden-grave`: Someone excluded from the official funeral tends an unmarked patch beside the wall and asks you not to ask whose name belongs there; mourning and withheld knowledge, tenderness; fund a private resting vigil or help carry stones for pay; Saturn: set one boundary stone and leave the name unspoken.
5. `hidden-scorpio-shut-drawer`: A retired investigator has nailed shut the drawer everyone assumes contains a secret, and now wants it carried somewhere she cannot see it; suspicion and deliberate closure, burden; take the heavy paid carry or hire help; Saturn: remove the drawer intact without testing its lid.

## Sagittarius

1. `hidden-sagittarius-border-hill`: A traveler forbidden to cross the border has climbed high enough to see the road continuing without him; open horizons made inaccessible, sorrow; take a paid cold watch beside him or buy shelter below; Saturn: choose one sheltered lookout and stop climbing higher.
2. `hidden-sagittarius-hermit-guide`: A hermit put up a sign welcoming anyone who could find him and now regrets the accuracy of his directions; frank invitation and desired freedom, comedy; buy a private resting hour or carry the sign down for pay; Saturn: turn it around for the afternoon he asked to keep.
3. `hidden-sagittarius-stable-room`: A person hiding from creditors sleeps above a stable and asks you to exercise the horse he cannot ride openly; horses and constrained movement, burden; take a short or long paid circuit; Saturn: use the enclosed yard for one useful session.
4. `hidden-sagittarius-too-many-stories`: An isolated traveler offers a fee to hear the account of every country he crossed, then admits he mostly wants somebody to stay; broad horizons and blunt admission, tenderness; listen briefly or through a paid night; Saturn: agree on one journey and a shared quiet hour afterward.
5. `hidden-sagittarius-far-window`: A retreat's largest window looks toward a famous peak, but its guest has covered it because he is tired of wanting to leave; distant aims and chosen limits, relief; purchase deep private rest or take the smaller free bench; Saturn: keep the view covered and enjoy the room's present shelter.

## Capricorn

1. `hidden-capricorn-frozen-lock`: An old exile's outside store is frozen shut, and the village locksmith will repair it only if someone brings the door to him; winter limits and denied service, burden; take the heavy paid removal or fund help; Saturn: warm the lock locally and leave the door hung.
2. `hidden-capricorn-old-workshop`: A retired maker keeps his workshop closed because every visitor brings something to mend, and asks whether your hands are empty; respected labor and private limits, dry humor; buy quiet time at the warm stove or accept a brief seat; Saturn: leave your damaged belongings outside before entering.
3. `hidden-capricorn-wall-seat`: A prisoner has polished one patch of the stone bench by sitting there for years and objects when the keeper offers a new seat; duration and a small domain, tenderness; pay for warmer bedding or carry a stone footrest for wages; Saturn: improve the familiar place without moving him.
4. `hidden-capricorn-no-record`: A clerk erased from the public rolls keeps the old seal wrapped in a cloth and pays you to inventory everything except it; authority lost and boundaries retained, tension; take a short or long sorting task; Saturn: set the wrapped seal outside the work's agreed boundary.
5. `hidden-capricorn-long-dark`: A retreat keeper has split the longest night into watches and left one deliberately unassigned so everyone may sleep; winter time and chosen restraint, relief; buy deep rest or take a paid watch; Saturn: leave the empty watch empty and secure the shutters together.

## Aquarius

1. `hidden-aquarius-attic-model`: A secluded engineer builds a model city with no doors and asks you not to interpret it before helping raise its heavy roof; independent thought and closed systems, humor; take the paid lift or buy quiet study time; Saturn: support the roof on blocks and lift only the loose section.
2. `hidden-aquarius-private-bath`: A communal bath has one screened basin for people who want water without conversation, and a committee keeps explaining its virtues through the screen; shared provision and individual distance, comedy; buy private recovery or use the public pool; Saturn: draw the screen and end the explanation.
3. `hidden-aquarius-unanswered-bell`: An isolated bell keeper has stopped answering signals whose meaning nobody has ever explained to him; fixed independence and impersonal systems, tension; take a paid cold watch or fund relief; Saturn: agree which single signal needs an answer during your watch.
4. `hidden-aquarius-quarry-room`: A quarry worker rents an abandoned cutting for solitude because its walls shut out the town, but rain collects on the only seat; uneven ground and chosen separation, practical burden; pay for dry shelter or haul a new bench for wages; Saturn: cut one shallow drainage groove before sitting.
5. `hidden-aquarius-hidden-circle`: A thinker excluded from her old society meets its newest members privately and asks you to leave their names off the visitor's slate; independent company under restriction, trust; purchase quiet study or help prepare the closed room for pay; Saturn: count places without recording identities.

## Pisces

1. `hidden-pisces-mill-room`: A person hiding in an unused mill likes the sound of the wheel but asks you to stop the leak above the bed; watery refuge and imperfect shelter, relief; buy deep rest downstairs or take the tiring paid repair; Saturn: divert the one leaking channel without silencing the wheel.
2. `hidden-pisces-porous-retreat`: A sanctuary guest came to be alone but keeps opening her door whenever she hears someone crying nearby; compassion without rest, tenderness; purchase private recovery or sit in the common room; Saturn: ask the keeper to cover one quiet hour while the door stays shut.
3. `hidden-pisces-missing-verse`: A confined singer asks you to carry her song into the courtyard, stopping before the verse she has not decided to share; music and guarded feeling, trust; sing a short paid part or a longer tiring one; Saturn: mark the stopping place and keep it.
4. `hidden-pisces-drifting-boat`: A solitary fisher has loosened his mooring just enough to drift beyond callers, but now cannot reach the bank's dry blankets; linked water and chosen withdrawal, comedy; row the paid long delivery or buy shelter ashore; Saturn: use a short line to bring the blankets within reach.
5. `hidden-pisces-unasked-prayer`: A retreat attendant keeps praying aloud for a guest who has asked only to sleep, and is embarrassed to be told he is awake; spiritual care and permeable boundaries, gentle conflict; buy a quiet bed or accept brief rest in the outer room; Saturn: take the attendant to the far chapel for one silent interval.

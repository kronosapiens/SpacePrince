# House IV scene briefs

These original situations follow [House IV](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iv--home) and its [planetary expressions](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#iv-home): shelter, household memory, private authority, and a place that can be occupied differently now.
The [sign notes](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) shape each household's people, season, and manner without assigning a personality to the Prince.
The four accepted scenes retain their IDs and choices from [SCENES.md](../SCENES.md).
Every scene includes a paid revival as an immediate welcome or return; this fantasy does not require someone in the opening to have literally died.
House IV has no joy, and ordinary participation leaves room for a free refusal or gift.

## Aries

1. `home-aries-first-fire` — Your sister insists the first meal in her new house belongs at its smoking hearth, while her coughing partner has already carried the child's bowl outside; beginnings and headlong authority, quarrel; accept her smoky hospitality, take on the exhausting flue work, or join the meal outside, with revival through opening the new home to someone waiting beyond the smoke.
2. `home-aries-cut-door` — An uncle wants to burn the timber cut from a new doorway for tonight's fire, but your sister grips the piece bearing the household's childhood height marks; forceful renewal against private history, dilemma; take the warmth, carry the heavy piece clear, or let the two keep arguing, with revival by opening the new doorway to someone who remembers the marks.
3. `home-aries-red-roof` — Your mother has started painting the roof a bright color before asking anyone, and holds out a brush when you arrive; spring building and direct authority, comic challenge; join the demanding work, pay for help and rest, or admire it from the ground, with revival by making the house visible to someone returning.
4. `home-aries-first-slice` — A brother claims the first slice from the household's new oven because he built it, while the aunt who taught him to bake holds the knife; first acts and heat, affectionate quarrel; accept either offered portion, help turn the next heavy batch, or let them settle the honor, with revival by putting another loaf into the cooling oven.
5. `home-aries-packed-room` — A cousin clearing her childhood room has dragged everything into the courtyard, then asks you to choose where she can sit down; initiative outrunning comfort, tender comedy; carry back one comfortable seat, help with the heavier rearrangement, or sit with her outside, with revival through making a place inside for someone who has stayed away.

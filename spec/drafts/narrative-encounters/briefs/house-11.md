# House XI briefs

[House XI](../../../concept/HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xi--friendship) supplies remembered ties, welcomed membership, and practical support for a shared intention.
[Sign references](../../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) distinguish the circles and their manner; [Jupiter in XI](../../../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#jupiter-xi--friendship) adds an opportunity through belonging rather than a future debt.
The possible gifts are used now, and any contribution buys an immediate effect; declining remains available.

## Aries

1. `friendship-aries-new-gate`: Friends offer you the completed garden gate’s first opening while the newest gardener waits outside, unsure whether she may enter before the ceremony; eager beginnings and membership, welcome; accept the opening purse or give her the first turn and join the rest inside; Jupiter: welcome every waiting member together.
2. `friendship-aries-spare-boots`: A walking group has pooled money for your new boots and argues cheerfully over who measured your feet wrong; initiative and familiar teasing, generosity; take the cash or restorative fitting; Jupiter: use their pooled credit to fit everyone whose boots hurt.
3. `friendship-aries-start-now`: Your friends have finally gathered to build the thing they discussed all winter, but the youngest has already begun in the wrong corner; spring impatience, comedy; take paid heavy work or fund proper tools; Jupiter: give each volunteer a useful part in the corrected start.
4. `friendship-aries-practice-yard`: A companion has reserved the practice yard for everyone who encouraged her first attempt and wants you to choose the opening exercise; direct action and gratitude, pleasure; choose vigorous Afflict practice or easy recovery; Jupiter: lead a mixed session that includes the hesitant newcomers.
5. `friendship-aries-lanterns`: Friends carry lanterns to meet you on the dark road and discover nobody brought enough oil for the walk home; bold welcome and poor preparation, affectionate burden; buy oil or carry the heavy reserve can for pay; Jupiter: combine their lamps into one bright escort.

## Taurus

2. `friendship-taurus-bread-credit`: A baker's friends have paid for bread in your name, and she asks whether to wrap it or warm it at the back table; practical abundance, generosity; take its cash equivalent or restorative meal; Jupiter: use the remaining group credit to open the table to others.
3. `friendship-taurus-strong-rope`: A gardening circle gives you its soundest rope for moving a stone, then waits to see whether you will ask for their hands as well; steadfast help and physical work, gentle invitation; do the paid pull alone or hire help; Jupiter: accept their offered strength together.
4. `friendship-taurus-quiet-song`: Your friends have decided you need a quiet welcome and arranged one song so quiet that the singer cannot hear the accompaniment; loyal affection and voice, comedy; sit for focused rest or join the musicians' close rehearsal; Jupiter: draw everyone close enough to sing comfortably.
5. `friendship-taurus-pottery`: A potter opens her studio to the circle that supported her kiln, offering each person one afternoon with whatever clay they want; craft and tangible generosity, pleasure; practice for Resolve or rest in the warm room; Jupiter: pool the afternoon into a shared making session.

## Gemini

3. `friendship-gemini-street-choir`: The neighborhood choir has two different sheets of music and is enjoying the disagreement too much to stop; paired voices and sociability, playful quarrel; join a demanding paid part or listen at rest; Jupiter: carry the refrain both versions share.
4. `friendship-gemini-copy-table`: Friends copying a public notice have left your place at the table and a purse for anyone whose hand lasts longest; letters and collective work, invitation; take a short paid turn or a long tiring one; Jupiter: rotate the writing with the willing hands around you.
5. `friendship-gemini-news-window`: A friend keeps the window open so travelers can add news to the circle's map, and your route is the last empty strip; roads and exchange, welcome; receive their small contribution or buy time for careful instruction; Jupiter: share your route while others fill in its missing connections.

## Virgo

## Libra

## Scorpio

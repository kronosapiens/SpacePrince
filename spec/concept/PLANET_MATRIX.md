# Planet and sign matrix

A reference for writing Space Prince's astrological copy.
Scope: seven planets, twelve signs, and all 84 planet–sign placements in Western astrology.
Sources consulted on 2026-09-07.

For house meanings and narrative encounter material, see the [house matrix](HOUSE_MATRIX.md).

Placements: [Sun](#sun-placements) · [Moon](#moon-placements) · [Mercury](#mercury-placements) · [Venus](#venus-placements) · [Mars](#mars-placements) · [Jupiter](#jupiter-placements) · [Saturn](#saturn-placements).

## Using this reference

Read the planet's framework, the sign's matrix row, and its placement entry together.
**Correspondences** record the symbolic vocabulary; **source notes** paraphrase particular authors; **synthesis and copy seeds** are our original editorial interpretations.
The seeds demonstrate possible writing directions and are not approved replacement copy.
Describe a part of the Prince that is becoming recognizable, with room for the player to identify with it.
Select a theme that suits the moment; an introduction need not end with a flaw or a lesson.

Each planet expresses its own domain rather than repeating a Sun portrait with a different name.
The existing [planet introductions](../../client/src/copy/planet-introductions.ts) are editorial work, not evidence for the matrix.
The [planetary chorus](PLANETS.md) supplies the game's literary voice separately.

## Planet frameworks

The domains below summarize the corresponding sections of [Wikipedia's Planets in astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology).
The authoring questions are our editorial interpretation.

| Planet | Glyph | Sourced domain | Authoring question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sun | ☉ | Identity, expression, vitality, authority | What feels recognizably mine? |
| Moon | ☽ | Feeling, instinct, memory, security | What lets me feel at home? |
| Mercury | ☿ | Thought, communication, learning, exchange | How do I understand and make myself understood? |
| Venus | ♀ | Affection, pleasure, beauty, relationship | What do I cherish, and how do I show it? |
| Mars | ♂ | Energy, courage, assertion, action | What moves me to act? |
| Jupiter | ♃ | Growth, exploration, belief, opportunity | What makes a larger life seem possible? |
| Saturn | ♄ | Time, responsibility, limits, endurance | What am I willing to sustain, and at what cost? |

Our Moon register emphasizes the intimate interior; Mercury, translation and the unexpected turn; Venus, appreciation and relation; Mars, chosen effort; Jupiter, generosity and meaning; Saturn, durable commitment and its limits.
These adaptations follow the game's [planetary archetypes](PLANETS.md), with the portraits written as personal introductions rather than quotations from the chorus.
Jupiter's opportunity is a narrative theme, not a promise of better game outcomes.

## Sun framework ☉

**Sourced domain:** conscious identity, expression, creative agency, vitality, pride, and authority. [Wikipedia, Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology#Sun)

**Developmental reading:** Cafe Astrology frames the Sun partly as a self one learns to express. [Cafe Astrology, opening paragraphs](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Traditional correspondences:** domicile in Leo; exaltation in Aries; detriment in Aquarius; fall in Libra. [Leo](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/leo.html), [Aries](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/aries.html), [Aquarius](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/aquarius.html), [Libra](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/libra.html), “Rulerships & Associations”

**Game interpretation:** the sovereign self, consistent with the [Sun's existing archetype](PLANETS.md#3-sun).
The authoring question is: *What makes this part of me feel alive and recognizably mine?*
A placement can express authority through care, craft, or imagination as readily as public leadership.
Treat dignity as symbolic context here; it does not rank a character's worth or prescribe their temperament.

**Original image prompts:** a light being uncovered; warmth returning to a room; a name spoken with conviction.
The glyph is a circle with a central dot; these narrative associations are our interpretation, not a claim about its historical origin.

## Sun placements

Everything labeled **synthesis**, **images**, or **copy seed** below is original editorial development from the cited themes.
These labels distinguish an author's interpretation from what we propose doing with it.

### Sun in Aries

**Source notes:** Houlding connects the ram and returning solar strength with renewal, initiative, and direct expression; haste can outrun sensitivity or endurance.
Locate the discussions of solar qualities and leadership. [Houlding, Aries](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/aries.html)
Cafe themes: initiative, independence, impatience. [Cafe, Aries](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity becomes tangible in an act of beginning.
Courage can give others permission to act; the tension is whether urgency leaves room for them.
**Images:** an unopened door; the first footprint; a match catching.
**Copy seed:** You may not know what waits beyond the door.
There is still something unmistakably yours in the hand that opens it.

### Sun in Taurus

**Source notes:** Houlding reads the bull through endurance, loyalty, grounded reassurance, and a capacity to resist movement.
Locate the passages on constancy and the sitting bull. [Houlding, Taurus](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/taurus.html)
Cafe themes: comfort, sensory pleasure, security. [Cafe, Taurus](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity gathers substance through tending and keeping.
Steadiness can make a life hospitable; attachment can preserve something after its usefulness has passed.
**Images:** a worn handle; soil pressed around roots; a table set again.
**Copy seed:** The things you tend begin to carry your presence.
A place can feel like home because you have returned to it with care.

### Sun in Gemini

**Source notes:** Houlding uses the twins to explore interdependence and contrasting experiences, connecting communication with movement between perspectives.
Locate the twin myth and the discussion of communication as a spiritual drive. [Houlding, Gemini](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/gemini.html)
Cafe themes: curiosity, adaptability, scattered attention. [Cafe, Gemini](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** exchange reveals possibilities within the self.
The gift is finding a language between different worlds; the tension is giving a conviction time to settle.
**Images:** two windows; an exchanged letter; a question answered with a story.
**Copy seed:** Someone asks a question you have never considered.
In answering, you hear something in your own voice that surprises you.

### Sun in Cancer

**Source notes:** Houlding connects the protective shell with a vulnerable interior, memory, belonging, and tenacious care.
Locate the opening shell image and later passages on personal history and holding on. [Houlding, Cancer](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/cancer.html)
Cafe themes: protection, remembrance, retreat. [Cafe, Cancer](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity takes shape around what is cherished.
Care can create shelter; fear of losing that shelter can make change feel disloyal.
**Images:** a saved letter; a familiar threshold; a cup kept for someone.
**Copy seed:** You remember how they take their tea, though they have been away for years.
Some forms of love live in what you keep ready.

### Sun in Leo

**Source notes:** Houlding connects the lion with solar presence, courage, generosity, and a place in which to shine, alongside sensitivity to perceived slights.
Locate the passages on the heart and one's personal kingdom. [Houlding, Leo](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/leo.html)
Cafe themes: warmth, loyalty, wanting appreciation. [Cafe, Leo](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity asks for a visible, heartfelt expression.
Being witnessed can nourish creative courage; recognition becomes constraining when it determines what feels worth making.
**Images:** a song shared aloud; an open curtain; a place made for a guest.
**Copy seed:** There is something you have made that only you could have offered.
Let someone see the pleasure you took in making it.

### Sun in Virgo

**Source notes:** Houlding emphasizes discernment, practical skill, and useful service, explicitly arguing that Virgo can balance standards against available resources.
Locate the passages distinguishing pragmatism from idealized perfection. [Houlding, Virgo](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/virgo.html)
Cafe themes: usefulness, mastery, perfectionism. [Cafe, Virgo](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Difference:** perfectionism is central to Cafe's portrait; Houlding challenges its treatment as an inevitable trait.
**Synthesis:** identity finds expression through attentive craft.
Include the satisfaction of sound judgment, as well as the possibility of endless correction.
**Images:** a well-fitted joint; sorted grain; a repaired seam.
**Copy seed:** You notice the small adjustment that lets everything work.
There is pleasure in knowing when a thing has received enough care.

### Sun in Libra

**Source notes:** Houlding reads the scales as active judgment and redress, with fairness sometimes requiring an uncomfortable stand.
Locate the opening discussions of equilibrium and taking a position. [Houlding, Libra](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/libra.html)
Cafe themes: cooperation, comparison, indecision. [Cafe, Libra](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity develops through encounters with another point of view.
The gift is making a fair relationship possible; the tension is confusing agreement with fairness.
**Images:** two chairs; a level balance; an agreement revised.
**Copy seed:** You can hear what each person is asking for.
Sometimes the fairest answer is the one that changes the conversation.

### Sun in Scorpio

**Source notes:** Houlding connects the scorpion's guarded power with persistence, emotional depth, and a demand for truth beneath appearances.
Locate the passages on falsity and truth, guardianship, and the fixed water element. [Houlding, Scorpio](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/scorpio.html)
Cafe themes: intensity, determination, private power. [Cafe, Scorpio](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity becomes clearer when something concealed can be acknowledged.
The gift is remaining present with difficult truths; the tension is expecting openness while keeping oneself inaccessible.
**Images:** a sealed letter; a deep well; a confession received quietly.
**Copy seed:** When the conversation reaches what no one wants to name, you can stay.
That steadiness may be the first thing that makes honesty possible.

### Sun in Sagittarius

**Source notes:** Houlding connects the archer's distant aim with broad vision, enthusiasm, candor, and the risk of exceeding practical limits.
Locate the opening discussion of Jupiter and the upward aim. [Houlding, Sagittarius](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/sagittarius.html)
Cafe themes: freedom, optimism, restlessness. [Cafe, Sagittarius](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity grows through a possibility larger than the familiar world.
The gift is making that possibility inviting; the tension is keeping promises when the initial excitement fades.
**Images:** an annotated map; a distant light; a story carried home.
**Copy seed:** An unfamiliar idea can make the world feel larger overnight.
You want to follow it far enough to return with a story worth telling.

### Sun in Capricorn

**Source notes:** Houlding pairs the goat's patient ascent with the sea tail's hidden depths, emphasizing inwardly sustained ambition and resilience.
Locate the opening climb and the passages explaining the goat-fish. [Houlding, Capricorn](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/capricorn.html)
Cafe themes: usefulness, responsibility, social recognition. [Cafe, Capricorn](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Difference:** Cafe stresses public recognition; Houlding stresses motivation that can persist without approval.
**Synthesis:** identity earns its own trust through sustained commitment.
Achievement may serve an inner purpose, with a tension between enduring responsibility and admitting personal limits.
**Images:** a sound foundation; a carefully chosen foothold; an old promise kept.
**Copy seed:** You return to the work long after anyone has thought to praise it.
What you are building has a meaning you do not need to announce.

### Sun in Aquarius

**Source notes:** Houlding reads the water bearer's offering as shared knowledge and service, combining independent thought with collective concern.
Locate the libation image and the discussion of fixed opinions. [Houlding, Aquarius](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/aquarius.html)
Cafe themes: independence, reform, fixed convictions. [Cafe, Aquarius](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity finds purpose in imagining how people could live differently together.
The gift is independent contribution; the tension is whether a cherished idea remains open to the people it would affect.
**Images:** a vessel passed around; a shared diagram; a chair added to a circle.
**Copy seed:** You can imagine a way of living together that has no place yet.
Giving the idea a voice lets someone else help it take shape.

### Sun in Pisces

**Source notes:** Houlding uses the connected fish to explore competing currents, receptivity, imagination, and difficulty sustaining one direction.
Locate the opening cord image and the passages on responsiveness to surrounding emotion. [Houlding, Pisces](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/pisces.html)
Cafe themes: compassion, imagination, porous boundaries. [Cafe, Pisces](https://cafeastrology.com/articles/suninsigns.html)

**Synthesis:** identity can accommodate experiences that resist clear definition.
The gift is imaginative sympathy; the tension is hearing one's own wishes amid everything one receives.
**Images:** two fish joined by a cord; a half-remembered melody; light moving through water.
**Copy seed:** A feeling can reach you before you have words for it.
Sometimes a melody gives it a shape you can finally recognize as your own.

## Moon placements

### Moon in Aries

**Source notes:** Immediate, intense feelings, open enthusiasm, and a restless appetite for change. [Astrology.com, Moon in Aries](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-aries)

**Synthesis:** Feelings ask for an immediate outlet.
Candor can clear the air, while haste can make a passing mood govern the moment.
**Images:** a quickened step; a window thrown open.
**Copy seed:** You can feel the answer before you can explain it.
A little movement gives the feeling somewhere to go.

### Moon in Taurus

**Source notes:** Comfort, sensory satisfaction, dependable attachment, and resistance to changed habits. [Astrology.com, Moon in Taurus](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-taurus)

**Synthesis:** Familiar pleasures help the inner world settle.
Continuity comforts, but a changed routine need not mean that safety has gone.
**Images:** a familiar blanket; bread cooling.
**Copy seed:** The room settles around its familiar sounds.
For a moment, nothing more is needed than the comfort already here.

### Moon in Gemini

**Source notes:** Conversation and variety bring emotional relief; intellectualizing can leave feelings unresolved. [Astrology.com, Moon in Gemini](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-gemini)

**Synthesis:** Talking helps a feeling become recognizable.
An explanation can offer relief before the feeling has finished its work.
**Images:** a late conversation; a page of unfinished sentences.
**Copy seed:** You begin by describing your day.
Somewhere in the telling, you discover what has been troubling you.

### Moon in Cancer

**Source notes:** Emotional cycles, sensitivity, motivating attachments, and an idealized relationship with the past. [Astrology.com, Moon in Cancer](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-cancer)

**Synthesis:** Remembered care helps create an inner home.
The tension is allowing that home to change without treating the change as abandonment.
**Images:** an old lullaby; lamplight in a remembered room.
**Copy seed:** A familiar voice can reach a place in you that argument cannot.
You remember being safe before you remember why.

### Moon in Leo

**Source notes:** Affection, encouragement, joyful expression, and private emotional drama. [Astrology.com, Moon in Leo](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-leo)

**Synthesis:** Warmth becomes believable when it is expressed.
The wish to brighten a room can leave little space to admit one's own sadness.

When someone joins in, the happiness feels more fully yours.

### Moon in Virgo

**Source notes:** Routine and organization offer peace; close attention can become persistent fault-finding. [Astrology.com, Moon in Virgo](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-virgo)

**Synthesis:** Small useful acts give unease a manageable shape.
The tension is knowing when the feeling needs company rather than another correction.
**Images:** a cleared surface; a carefully folded cloth.
**Copy seed:** Putting one small thing in order helps you breathe.
There can be comfort in a task gentle enough to finish.

### Moon in Libra

**Source notes:** Companionship, social goodwill, and a risk of neglecting personal needs to win approval. [Astrology.com, Moon in Libra](https://www.astrology.com/planets/moon-in-libra)

**Synthesis:** Ease between people helps the inner world rest.

### Moon in Scorpio

### Moon in Sagittarius

### Moon in Capricorn

**Synthesis:** Dependability can steady the inner world.

### Moon in Aquarius

## Mercury placements

### Mercury in Aries

**Source notes:** Passionate direct speech, quick expression, and a tendency to miss nuance or other speakers' reactions. [Astrology.com, Mercury in Aries](https://www.astrology.com/planets/mercury-in-aries)

**Synthesis:** Thought becomes clearer in a first, decisive statement.
The tension is giving a correction as much attention as the original insight.
**Images:** a first sketch; a sharply penciled question.
**Copy seed:** You find the words that get the conversation moving.
They need not be the last words on the subject.

### Mercury in Taurus

**Source notes:** Measured judgments, sensory evidence, practical communication, and reluctance to revise a conclusion. [Astrology.com, Mercury in Taurus](https://www.astrology.com/planets/mercury-in-taurus)

**Synthesis:** Understanding grows through concrete examples and time to consider them.
A settled judgment can become harder to reopen than it was to reach.
**Images:** a worked example; a stone turned in the hand.
**Copy seed:** You want to see how an idea holds up in use.
Once it makes sense in your hands, you can explain it plainly.

### Mercury in Gemini

# House and planet research pilot

Proposed revisions for the eighteen placements selected in [issue #9](https://github.com/kronosapiens/SpacePrince/issues/9): Saturn in all twelve houses, plus the other six classical planets in Achievement.
This pilot sits alongside the [84-entry matrix](HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md) for review before work on the remaining 66 entries.

## Reading the pilot

The house supplies a situation and its stakes; the occupying planet supplies a concern or form of agency; the sign shapes its expression.
Occupancy refers to the Prince's own chart, separately from the house's fixed ruler and planetary joy.
Under whole-sign houses, planets sharing a house also share its sign.

All dynamics, expressions, and scenes below are original game adaptations.
The two expressions show possible wants and responses, rather than fixed personalities or opposite moral outcomes.
The [drives guidance](DRIVES.md) distinguishes these motives from the player's experience.
Scenes propose immediate narrative decisions; their costs, benefits, eligibility, and implementation remain separate work.

The [Saturn notes](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md) and [Achievement notes](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md) identify consulted passages, disagreements, and limitations.
The [review report](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_PILOT_REVIEW.md) contains comparisons, independent critique, fresh scene exercises, and an assessment of the method.

## Saturn across the houses

### Saturn I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants possession of his own bearing before another person decides how he should appear.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy composure; he can also resent the effort of maintaining it and want someone else to take care of him.

**Scenes**

- A tailor keeps straightening his shoulders while fitting a plain traveling coat.
  He can hold the imposed posture or ask her to fit the body as it rests.
- A barber offers to wash the road dust from his hair, but he keeps reaching up to direct her hands.
  He can specify each movement or settle back and let himself be handled.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#i--self), especially Alice Sparkly Kat's discussion of control and composure.

### Saturn II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants provision he can rely on without feeling owned by its provider.
**Expressions:** He can take satisfaction in making his supplies last; he can suspect dependence even when help comes freely.

**Scenes**

- An innkeeper offers bread for the road without asking for anything.
  The Prince can take it, insist on paying, or offer a small repair so he can call the exchange settled.
- A seller offers to replace his battered provision tin, whose lid still seals perfectly.
  He can keep the familiar tin with quiet satisfaction or accept the replacement without rehearsing every way it could fail.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).
The imagined obligation in the first scene is his concern, not a concealed debt.

### Saturn III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to speak among familiar people without every word having to serve as an example.
**Expressions:** He can lend weight to a carefully considered message; he can use silence or dry humor to escape being the dependable authority.

**Scenes**

- A younger cousin repeats his old advice just as he is about to contradict it.
  He can defend it, admit he has changed his mind, or answer with a joke whose irritation remains audible.
- A neighbor asks him to dictate a complaint because everyone takes his words seriously.
  He can state the grievance precisely or decline to turn her annoyance into his pronouncement.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iii--communication).
The authority comes from repeated local contact, rather than a public office.

### Saturn IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants continuity with a household whose familiar habits can also confine him.
**Expressions:** He can cherish an old arrangement without calling it happy; he can change one domestic habit without repudiating the whole home.

**Scenes**

- His old stool remains beside the hearth, where he used to sit through family scoldings.
  He can take the seat he missed or move it into the circle without explaining why.
- The household still bolts the inner doors before supper, a rule he has always disliked.
  Tonight the key is in his hand: he can repeat the routine or leave the courtyard open while everyone eats.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iv--home).
These are small acts within an inherited way of living, not instant reconciliation.

### Saturn V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to claim his limited time for a pleasure he takes seriously.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an exacting pastime for its own sake; he can hunger for play because too much time already feels lost, even while fearing foolishness.

**Scenes**

- An elderly dancer remembers a difficult step no one else requests anymore.
  The Prince can ask her to repeat it with him, enjoying the attempt without an audience or a finished performance.
- The fair is closing, and a puppeteer offers him one turn behind the little curtain.
  He can give the puppet an absurd voice or return it with the excuse that he is too old for this.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#v--creativity).
Skill, embarrassment, and appetite belong to the pleasure itself; neither seed needs a useful product.

### Saturn VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the necessary work done on terms he can continue to bear.
**Expressions:** He can find satisfaction in reliable practice; he can remain committed while resenting the task or the person directing it.

**Scenes**

- A cooper needs the water casks readied before supper.
  The Prince can take the familiar task of setting their hoops, enjoy the practiced rhythm, and finish with the last cask ready.
- A replacement arrives for his turn at the wash trough and begins stacking the bowls differently.
  He can leave the work in those hands or stay to redo it, irritated that relief does not look like relief.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vi--labor).
Reed's enjoyment of service and Alice Sparkly Kat's dedication without enjoyment remain different possibilities.

### Saturn VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a bond dependable enough that he need not keep proving himself a flawless partner.
**Expressions:** He can take pleasure in a particular person's reliability; he can protect the agreement so closely that ordinary ease together starts to feel like another obligation.

**Scenes**

- At the end of their agreed journey, his guide invites him to supper with no further work attached.
  He can accept as company or ask what the invitation requires, making his unease part of their conversation.
- A companion returns the borrowed cloak exactly when promised, still damp from the rain.
  He can insist that returning it dry was part of the bargain or wrap it around both of them while they wait by the fire.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).
The other person's stated terms matter; this is not a test of whether the Prince learns to trust everyone.

### Saturn VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to decide which claims survive a loss.
**Expressions:** He can accept a material loss to honor a chosen obligation; he can refuse a legacy that would give the dead continuing authority over his life.

**Scenes**

- An executor divides the last of an estate while someone the deceased supported waits outside the list of heirs.
  The Prince can include them in his portion or take the division as written, with affection and resentment still unsettled.
- A bequest is offered on condition that he sign the dead benefactor's account of their quarrel as true.
  He can sign for the inheritance or leave it unclaimed and keep his disagreement.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
The disputed claim and its settlement occur here; neither response creates a later tracked obligation or makes the death beneficial.

### Saturn IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a belief or practice that remains credible beyond the people who first taught it to him.
**Expressions:** He can test a teaching patiently; he can hold to an observance after confidence in its explanation has worn away.

**Scenes**

- At a distant navigation school, advanced students applaud a shortcut that skips the first measurement.
  He can request the omitted demonstration, from curiosity or the hope of puncturing the teacher's reputation.
- A shrine keeper explains that the bowl he has carried uphill is customary, not required by the doctrine he learned at home.
  He can pour his offering anyway or set the still-full bowl aside, disappointed or relieved.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage).
The teaching has a subject, and doubt need not end in either conversion or ridicule.

### Saturn X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants an earned public place to endure without having to justify his presence forever.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy authority that is finally trusted; he can cling to the undertaking and its people because handing it on feels like erasure.

**Scenes**

- After years of annual demonstrations, the guild grants him a permanent master's seal.
  He can accept it without adding another proof, or ask whether the old examiner has really agreed.
- At the opening of the observatory he directed, his collaborators are already packing to leave and a successor waits for the keys.
  He can complete the handover or call everyone back to hear his account before releasing them.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Saturn research, X](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#x--achievement) and [Achievement research, Saturn](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#saturn-in-achievement).
Public charge gives these attachments their setting; company alone would belong to Friendship.

### Saturn XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ties that can survive silence without continual proof of affection.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy being remembered by a few people; he can sustain a common effort while keeping his private life to himself.

**Scenes**

- Old companions have saved his place despite receiving no letters from him.
  He can sit down as though no explanation is owed or tell them why he stayed away.
- Friends ask him to sign a shared petition and tell the gathering the private experience behind his support.
  He can sign without telling the story, speak about it, or withdraw his name because the request has changed what joining means.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).
These people seek his participation as a member, not his authority as an officeholder.

### Saturn XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants some say over his separation from others.
**Expressions:** He can choose unobserved time without explaining or improving himself; he can anticipate judgment so thoroughly that an available contact feels foreclosed.

**Scenes**

- From a retreat room he hears the visitors gathering outside and rehearses every question they might ask.
  He can open the door, ask for one visitor, or leave it closed without the narration deciding whether this is rest or avoidance.
- Confined to a gatehouse, he is offered a supervised visit but must speak through the keeper.
  He can insist on delivering his own short message or refuse the visit rather than have the keeper speak for him.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
The confinement seed combines Houlding's house account with Saturnian restraint; it is an original synthesis, not a direct placement claim.

## Achievement across the planets

All seven can seek recognition; what recognition permits or satisfies distinguishes them.
These contrasts summarize the proposals, not exclusive planetary territories.

| Planet | What the Prince wants from public standing |
| --- | --- |
| Sun | Authorship and the right to act in his own name |
| Moon | Recognition that feels personally sustaining |
| Mercury | A voice that changes the accepted account |
| Venus | Distinction he enjoys inhabiting |
| Mars | Freedom to pursue and prevail |
| Jupiter | Scope for an undertaking he believes in |
| [Saturn](#saturn-x--achievement) | An earned place that lasts beyond another examination |

### Sun X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants public action to carry his authorship, including the authority to say what it means.
**Expressions:** He can take pleasure in an honor as his own; he can refuse a prestigious role that makes him another person's emblem.

**Scenes**

- At the unveiling of a fountain he designed, the crowd calls for its maker.
  He can step forward, name the part he is proudest of, and enjoy the applause.
- The council asks him to announce its judgment in his own name, although he disagrees with it.
  He can read it as the council's messenger or state his own judgment and decline to lend his name to theirs.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Sun](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#sun-in-achievement).

### Moon X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** Public success matters to the Prince through the people and memories that make it feel like acceptance.
**Expressions:** He can savor being welcomed back; he can resent the familiar role an audience still needs him to play.

**Scenes**

- The town welcomes him as the child who once rang its festival bell and asks him to perform the part again as its honored guest.
  He can repeat the old flourish or accept the welcome while asking to be introduced as he is now.
- His former teacher rises to leave just before his insignia is presented.
  He can interrupt the ceremony to ask her to fasten it or face the officials and receive it without her.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Moon](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#moon-in-achievement).
The public occasion exposes a private attachment; it does not require the crowd to be wrong about him.

### Mercury X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a voice that can change what publicly counts as an achievement.
**Expressions:** He can relish a precise distinction that changes an official account; he can resist being confined to the one expertise for which he is already known.

**Scenes**

- A herald calls him the discoverer of a passage he learned from local guides.
  He can replace “discovered” with “charted,” choosing an accurate claim that still makes his contribution visible.
- Asked to judge maps, he notices that the council's musicians need someone to explain an unfamiliar notation.
  He can remain the recognized map expert or demonstrate that the same interpretive skill earns him a different hearing.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Mercury](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#mercury-in-achievement).
These follow different source emphases: command of a formal account and appetite for changing work.

### Venus X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants distinction to be delightful to inhabit.
**Expressions:** He can savor admiration without asking for intimacy; he can want others to love what delights him, using his standing to shape a shared occasion.

**Scenes**

- The guild offers him a ceremonial mantle in a severe color and a bright one it considers less dignified.
  He can choose the bright mantle and enjoy being admired in something he actually likes.
- Town singers choose him to lead their open rehearsal because travelers have carried his songs from village to village.
  He can choose his favorite bawdy round and draw out its pauses, relishing the singers following his cues in the public square.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Venus](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#venus-in-achievement).
Shaping the rehearsal through an admired reputation is our scene development; charm does not establish dishonesty.

### Mars X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the authority to attempt something decisive in his own way.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy prevailing against a rival; he can refuse an honor that leaves him unable to act.

**Scenes**

- Two candidates for harbor pilot are invited to demonstrate their course through a scale model of the shoals.
  He can demand the difficult approach first, wanting the appointment and the pleasure of beating the other candidate.
- At the harbor, the expedition's patron receives the public credit but gives the Prince command of departure.
  He can send a shallow launch through the near channel to test his chosen route or follow the harbor master's slower convoy, leaving the cheers to the patron.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Mars](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#mars-in-achievement).
Competitive appetite and command give these scenes their force without requiring bodily strain or a righteous opponent.

### Jupiter X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a public position large enough for what he imagines doing through it.
**Expressions:** He can accept an opening with delighted confidence; he can relish granting recognition and making his vision the measure of what deserves support.

**Scenes**

- An academy invites him to preside over a public demonstration and lets him choose its subject.
  He can give the familiar lecture or unfold the ambitious account he has never had an audience large enough to attempt.
- Asked to award a travel fellowship, he favors an applicant whose proposed journey extends his own unfinished exploration.
  He can name that applicant with sincere excitement or choose another whose promise leads somewhere he would never have gone.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Jupiter](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#jupiter-in-achievement).
Making patronage serve his own vision is an original extension, with thinner direct support than the source themes of advancement and worthwhile work.

## Selected sign variations

These original combinations use the existing planet–sign interpretations, not newly researched readings of all three factors together.
Each pair varies one scene from the pilot while retaining its house and planetary concern.
The variants describe different possible charts; two planets occupying one house in the same chart would share a sign.

| Placement and reference | Variation |
| --- | --- |
| [Saturn in Cancer](PLANET_MATRIX.md#saturn-in-cancer), V | He asks for the step his family used to dance, then lets the older dancer guide him through the part he never learned. |
| [Saturn in Aquarius](PLANET_MATRIX.md#saturn-in-aquarius), V | He asks the dancer to help devise a version two seated players could enjoy, treating their shared experiment as seriously as the old step. |
| [Mercury in Virgo](PLANET_MATRIX.md#mercury-in-virgo), X | He marks the precise stretch he surveyed so the herald can distinguish charting from discovery. |
| [Mercury in Gemini](PLANET_MATRIX.md#mercury-in-gemini), X | He asks the herald and a guide to tell the same passage's story in turn, then proposes a description that holds both accounts. |
| [Venus in Cancer](PLANET_MATRIX.md#venus-in-cancer), X | He chooses a mantle lined with cloth from a cherished old garment, enjoying public distinction that still feels personally familiar. |
| [Venus in Aquarius](PLANET_MATRIX.md#venus-in-aquarius), X | He wears the guild's bright, unconventional mantle without asking his fellow honorees to match, pleased by the contrast in the ceremonial row. |

These sketches test differences in expression, not relative strength, rarity, or rewards.

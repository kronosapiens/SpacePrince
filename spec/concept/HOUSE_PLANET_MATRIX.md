# House and planet matrix

A researched authoring reference for all 84 combinations of seven planets and twelve houses in Space Prince.
Each entry gives the Prince a central want, two possible expressions, two scene seeds, and a link to the source reasoning.
The [house reference](HOUSE_MATRIX.md) supplies situations and their structural character; the [planet and sign reference](PLANET_MATRIX.md) supplies planetary domains and ways of expressing them.
The [research review](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_REVIEW.md) records comparisons, independent checks, fresh writing exercises, and limitations.

Houses: [I Self](#i-self) · [II Livelihood](#ii-livelihood) · [III Communication](#iii-communication) · [IV Home](#iv-home) · [V Creativity](#v-creativity) · [VI Labor](#vi-labor) · [VII Relationships](#vii-relationships) · [VIII Transformation](#viii-transformation) · [IX Pilgrimage](#ix-pilgrimage) · [X Achievement](#x-achievement) · [XI Friendship](#xi-friendship) · [XII The Hidden](#xii-the-hidden).

## Reading the entries

The house supplies the situation; the occupying planet supplies a concern or form of agency; the sign shapes its expression.
This matrix is indexed by **House + Planet**; the Prince's natal chart determines which of its 84 pairings are occupied.
Each house introduction includes its [fixed House attributes](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#house-structure), such as angularity and favorability, once for all seven entries.
The occupant's Sign supplies expression through the separate Planet–Sign reference, and that pairing also determines dignity.
The [encounter lookup model](../mechanics/HOUSES.md#3-chart-conditioning-model) and [composition guidance](../mechanics/ENCOUNTERS.md#46-composing-and-revising-a-scenario) describe how these references contribute to options and economic terms.
The Prince takes the action and can want pleasure, company, power, possession, recognition, relief, or an answer without becoming an example of good conduct.
The [drives guidance](DRIVES.md) distinguishes his motives from the player's experience.
Its vocabulary remains provisional; the entries do not allocate each planet a single drive or require every scene to end in growth.

**Dynamic** states what matters to him here.
**Expressions** offer different possibilities, not fixed personalities or opposite moral outcomes.
**Scenes** make the concern concrete without specifying prices, rewards, or a correct choice.
All three are original game adaptations.
A scene can end with uncomplicated enjoyment, an unresolved feeling, or a refusal.

Occupancy refers to the Prince's own chart, separately from the house's fixed ruler and planetary joy.
The fixed ruler supplies the encounter's color and musical identity; a joy is a separate affinity.
Under whole-sign houses, planets sharing a house also share its sign.
The sign variations below describe possible different charts.

## Source basis

Research completed on 2026-10-04 includes direct contemporary placement readings for every entry and selected historical comparisons.
The notes identify authors, passage locators, conditions, disagreements, and inaccessible material.
A historical prediction supplies neither a psychological motive nor a promise of a game outcome.
These readings are literary reference material, not evidence that chart placements cause personalities.

## I Self

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#i--self): presence, bodily life, and how a person introduces themselves.
The encounter concerns inhabiting an identity before it becomes a public reputation.
His own gesture can establish how the encounter proceeds.

### Sun I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to recognize himself in the person he presents.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy taking up space; he can want an altered appearance accepted without accounting for the change.

**Scenes**

- A bath attendant offers washable gold to accent the marks on his skin.
  He can choose the scar he likes, stand undecorated, or ask to see his whole reflection before deciding.
- Every finished mask in the maker's stall gives him a younger face.
  He can enjoy borrowing one for the evening or sit while she shapes the unpainted clay to his face as it is.

**Basis:** [Sun research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Moon I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants room for what his body reveals before he has chosen an introduction.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy arriving visibly delighted; he can resent being asked to explain a sadness already audible in his voice.

**Scenes**

- A host opens the door wearing the absurd hat he remembers from childhood.
  He can surrender his formal entrance to laughter or make his bow with the laugh still escaping.
- A registrar hears his voice break while asking his name and leaves the pen hovering.
  He can repeat the name, acknowledge the pause, or ask her to write without further explanation.

**Basis:** [Moon research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Mercury I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to discover how he can present himself through a quick response.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an improvised introduction; he can resent being so familiar that somebody else supplies his answer.

**Scenes**

- A host introduces travelers through riddles and leaves his ending unfinished.
  He can invent a reply that makes him curious to inhabit the person it suggests.
- An acquaintance greets him with a practiced imitation of his usual flourish.
  He can answer with an imitation of theirs or try a disarmingly plain greeting neither expects.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Venus I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants pleasure in his own presence, including the way it attracts another person's attention.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy being appealing; he can tire of the agreeable manner through which people first welcomed him.

**Scenes**

- A tailor offers a coat lining visible only when he turns.
  He can choose the startling red and enjoy the flourish without waiting for anyone to praise it.
- A host introduces him as someone who never objects.
  He can enjoy the flattering welcome or tell the next guest one preference the introduction erased.

**Basis:** [Venus research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Mars I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to meet the situation through an act of his own before a role is assigned to him.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an immediate bodily attempt; he can become impatient with even a flattering introduction that delays participation.

**Scenes**

- A riding instructor asks what sort of rider he is.
  He can choose a mount and show the turn he wants to try, pleased to answer by moving.
- At a gathering, an usher rehearses where he should stand and when he may enter.
  He can step forward as the door opens or accept the offered cue, feeling the effort of waiting.

**Basis:** [Mars research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Jupiter I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to arrive as someone for whom more is possible than his present circumstances suggest.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy expansive confidence; he can enjoy the expectations his entrance raises before earning them.

**Scenes**

- A host asks each traveler for an introduction.
  He can name the undertaking he hopes to attempt as well as the modest work he has actually done, letting the room meet his ambition.
- An attendant offers him a robe grander than anything he owns for the evening's welcome.
  He can wear it with delighted ease or use its loose sleeves for a sweeping greeting that makes the others laugh.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Saturn I — Self

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants possession of his own bearing before another person decides how he should appear.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy composure; he can also resent the effort of maintaining it and want someone else to take care of him.

**Scenes**

- A tailor keeps straightening his shoulders while fitting a plain traveling coat.
  He can hold the imposed posture or ask her to fit the body as it rests.
- A barber offers to wash the road dust from his hair, but he keeps reaching up to direct her hands.
  He can specify each movement or settle back and let himself be handled.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#i--self), especially Alice Sparkly Kat's discussion of control and composure.

## II Livelihood

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ii--livelihood): usable possessions, income, provisions, and material sufficiency.
Resources here sustain the living traveler; an inheritance belongs to the Eighth and a shared undertaking to the Eleventh.
An offer can leave choices about acquisition or retention even when resources are abundant.

### Sun II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his possessions to give substance to his sense of capability and worth.
**Expressions:** He can take pride in what he has earned; he can feel diminished by a perfectly useful possession that fails to represent him.

**Scenes**

- The bowls he made have sold, and the seller hands him the proceeds in a plain cloth.
  He can count them with pleasure or buy the purse he has imagined carrying his own earnings in.
- A merchant offers sound used boots beside an expensive pair stamped with a maker's prestigious mark.
  He can choose either, admitting whether he wants the walking or the wearing to feel different.

**Basis:** [Sun research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Moon II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his provisions to make him feel supplied, as well as keep him supplied.
**Expressions:** He can savor a full bag; he can spend on immediate comfort when another prudent purchase would leave the day just as bleak.

**Scenes**

- A provisioner offers plain traveling food or a smaller supply of the spiced bread he remembers waking to.
  He can choose either, knowing exactly how many meals each provides.
- His meal is paid for, but he keeps checking the untouched reserve in his pouch.
  He can ask to have tomorrow's bread wrapped now or leave the coins where they are and finish eating.

**Basis:** [Moon research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Mercury II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his understanding of things to improve what he can obtain from an exchange.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy finding an overlooked use; he can insist on time to appraise something before accepting another person's valuation.

**Scenes**

- A provisioner rejects his cracked basin, but its halves would fit two leaking grain chutes.
  He can demonstrate that use and bargain for provisions on a different basis.
- A buyer keeps raising the offer for his tool while saying little about its maker's mark.
  He can take the tempting price or ask to examine the shop's catalogue before naming his own.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Venus II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his resources to furnish a life he actually enjoys.
**Expressions:** He can spend on a wanted pleasure; he can give generously while hoping his taste or contribution will be appreciated.

**Scenes**

- With tomorrow's provisions bought, he has enough left for fragrant soap or another spare strap.
  He can buy the scent he has wanted since the last town and enjoy using it tonight.
- He buys a basket of fine peaches for the travelers' table and watches them disappear without comment.
  He can ask whether anyone noticed the variety or take the last peach for himself.

**Basis:** [Venus research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Mars II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the power to acquire and use what he wants on his own terms.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy securing the means of his next undertaking; he can guard a possession simply because it is his.

**Scenes**

- A trader offers payment or ownership of the tool he has repaired.
  He can take the tool and begin his own work immediately, or take the money for a different desire.
- A traveler asks to borrow his new knife before he has used it.
  He can lend it, propose an exchange, or keep the first cut for himself without inventing a prudent reason.

**Basis:** [Mars research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Jupiter II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants provision ample enough to widen the life he can choose.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy having more than today's necessities; he can want abundance because modest sufficiency feels too small for his plans.

**Scenes**

- Payment is offered in coins or a whole crate of fruit.
  He can take the crate, eat freely, and invite the people nearby to choose some without calculating a return.
- A provisioner opens a larger pack with room for the supplies of several possible routes.
  He can fill it for the pleasure of keeping those possibilities open or choose the provisions for one definite road.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Saturn II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants provision he can rely on without feeling owned by its provider.
**Expressions:** He can take satisfaction in making his supplies last; he can suspect dependence even when help comes freely.

**Scenes**

- An innkeeper offers bread for the road without asking for anything.
  The Prince can take it, insist on paying, or offer a small repair so he can call the exchange settled.
- A seller offers to replace his battered provision tin, whose lid still seals perfectly.
  He can keep the familiar tin with quiet satisfaction or accept the replacement without rehearsing every way it could fail.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).
The imagined obligation in the first scene is his concern, not a concealed debt.

## III Communication

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iii--communication): neighbors, siblings, everyday messages, and familiar short journeys.
An ordinary exchange is the immediate stake, even when it carries affection or responsibility.
Practical help can be close at hand while the exchange depends on another person's response.

### Sun III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his own account to matter among the people who know him.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy being sought for an explanation; he can resent a familiar person's power to puncture his version of events.

**Scenes**

- A new neighbor asks why the market signal changes halfway through the morning.
  He can demonstrate the custom or relish telling the ridiculous incident that made it necessary.
- His sister interrupts a favorite childhood story with a detail that changes his part in it.
  He can defend his telling or invite hers, still wanting the listeners to understand why he remembers it differently.

**Basis:** [Sun research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Moon III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ordinary words and shared habits to carry a feeling of connection.
**Expressions:** He can delight in a shared verbal habit; he can shape ordinary news to invite reassurance or keep someone from worrying.

**Scenes**

- Two neighboring stalls trade a morning call, but the new seller does not know the answering line.
  The Prince can supply the familiar answer or help the newcomer make one that fits today.
- His brother's messenger waits for an account of a minor mishap.
  He can describe it briskly or include the small detail that will tell his brother he was frightened.

**Basis:** [Moon research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Mercury III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the next detail or discovery moving through the world he knows.
**Expressions:** He can relish news without needing to settle it; he can test a familiar explanation that no longer accounts for what he notices.

**Scenes**

- Three neighbors give incompatible accounts of a runaway goat.
  He can invite the least likely version to continue, enjoying the story's growth without declaring it true.
- The village pipes tap the same strange sequence whenever the mill stops.
  He can recruit a listener at the other end and tap back, curious whether the familiar system behaves as people say.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#iii--communication) and [Firmicus's discovery passage](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#mercury-in-iii).
The pipe experiment is an original local situation, not a historical example.

### Venus III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ordinary words to make him someone a familiar listener is pleased to hear from.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy making a familiar person laugh; he can flatter because he wants their attention to return to him.

**Scenes**

- His sister asks him to finish a teasing rhyme about her new admirer.
  He can make her laugh or sharpen the last line enough for his envy to show.
- A neighbor repeats his old compliment every time they meet.
  He can repeat the pleasing ritual or offer a more particular observation and wait for a new reply.

**Basis:** [Venus research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Mars III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to answer familiar people directly, with enough force to change the exchange.
**Expressions:** He can relish an argument both parties enjoy; he can insist on confronting the person behind a circulating complaint.

**Scenes**

- His brother disputes every coin in his account of yesterday's market bargain.
  He can argue back for the pleasure of their contest or concede the price while demanding another round over the quality.
- A neighbor has sent three increasingly sharp messages about noise from his lodging.
  He can knock and state his own complaint face to face or send one equally pointed reply.

**Basis:** [Mars research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Jupiter III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants familiar conversation to open a wider prospect, and likes being the person who offers it.
**Expressions:** He can delight in connecting neighbors' possibilities; he can give more advice than someone sought because being consulted pleases him.

**Scenes**

- Two neighboring shops each discard something the other buys.
  He can introduce the owners and enjoy hearing their exchange grow beyond the suggestion he brought.
- A younger cousin asks about one apprenticeship.
  He can answer or begin describing three more ambitious careers, then decide whether to make room for the cousin's narrower wish.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#iii--communication).
Enjoying influence is an original extension of the source's advisory role.

### Saturn III — Communication

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to speak among familiar people without every word having to serve as an example.
**Expressions:** He can lend weight to a carefully considered message; he can use silence or dry humor to escape being the dependable authority.

**Scenes**

- A younger cousin repeats his old advice just as he is about to contradict it.
  He can defend it, admit he has changed his mind, or answer with a joke whose irritation remains audible.
- A neighbor asks him to dictate a complaint because everyone takes his words seriously.
  He can state the grievance precisely or decline to turn her annoyance into his pronouncement.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iii--communication).
The authority comes from repeated local contact, rather than a public office.

## IV Home

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iv--home): shelter, roots, household memory, land, and private endings.
Care here concerns a place and its history, rather than every instance of comfort or kindness.
Private authority matters: establish who can offer shelter and who sets its immediate terms.

### Sun IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his home to express the life he regards as his own.
**Expressions:** He can take pride in an inherited foundation; he can give a household a form that his relatives would not have chosen.

**Scenes**

- Relatives bring the old family banner for his newly repaired front room.
  He can hang it centrally or place his traveling drawings there, making room for the banner elsewhere.
- Showing a guest his childhood room, he still calls one corner forbidden, although its cupboard is now empty.
  He can put his own belongings there or leave the corner untouched, recognizing the choice as his.

**Basis:** [Sun research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Moon IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a dwelling whose ordinary habits answer his present need to belong.
**Expressions:** He can welcome being cared for without explanation; he can want a different room even in a household he loves.

**Scenes**

- His former guardian has prepared the bed beside the kitchen because he once disliked sleeping alone.
  He can settle there gratefully or ask for the unfamiliar upstairs room.
- At the first supper in a borrowed house, he begins placing the bowls in the arrangement his family used.
  He can finish the familiar pattern or move his own bowl beside the person he wants near tonight.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Moon research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#iv--home) and [Venus research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--moon-iv).

### Mercury IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to think through a household's stories and possibilities with the people who live there.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy thinking aloud among familiar people; he can want to understand a family object whose history no longer seems settled.

**Scenes**

- Over supper, the household debates where to put a new window, drawing increasingly extravagant designs in spilled flour.
  He can add a plan or turn one around to see how another resident imagines the room.
- Behind an ancestral portrait he finds the name of another household.
  He can ask an elder how it came here or turn the frame so both names remain visible while they talk.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Venus IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants his dwelling to give tangible room to the people and pleasures he cherishes.
**Expressions:** He can delight in preparing a particular welcome; he can become possessive about who occupies a cherished domestic place.

**Scenes**

- Someone he loves is arriving after dark and has always liked supper beside the open courtyard.
  He can set their dishes there and enjoy anticipating the evening together.
- Returning home, he finds his favorite courtyard corner prepared for a relative's new companion.
  He can join their supper or ask for his old place, though the newcomer has already begun to settle in.

**Basis:** [Venus research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Mars IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants an active say over the space in which he lives.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy changing a room with his own hands; he can want to prevail over another resident's equally ordinary preference.

**Scenes**

- The sleeping alcove has long been divided by a heavy screen, and he wants morning light on his bed.
  With the room now his, he can move it immediately or try the bed on the other side first.
- His cousin keeps moving the courtyard table into the shade, while he wants supper in the last sun.
  He can move it back, propose two small tables, or give way while still wanting his arrangement.

**Basis:** [Mars research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Jupiter IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a household spacious enough for the way of living he believes in.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy enlarging its welcome; he can want its customs to embody his ideals even when another resident disagrees.

**Scenes**

- An adjoining room becomes available for the family gathering, and more relatives are waiting outside.
  He can open it with pleasure and draw them into the household for the night.
- He wants every guest to join the blessing with which his family begins supper, but one asks to keep a different observance.
  He can give them room at the table or explain why he wanted the whole household to speak together.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Saturn IV — Home

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants continuity with a household whose familiar habits can also confine him.
**Expressions:** He can cherish an old arrangement without calling it happy; he can change one domestic habit without repudiating the whole home.

**Scenes**

- His old stool remains beside the hearth, where he used to sit through family scoldings.
  He can take the seat he missed or move it into the circle without explaining why.
- The household still bolts the inner doors before supper, a rule he has always disliked.
  Tonight the key is in his hand: he can repeat the routine or leave the courtyard open while everyone eats.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iv--home).
These are small acts within an inherited way of living, not instant reconciliation.

## V Creativity

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#v--creativity): play, pleasure, children, courtship, and making something for its own sake.
The activity can matter without producing useful goods or winning public recognition.
He can sustain an occasion, join in, or enjoy what others have made.

### Sun V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the delight of bringing something unmistakably his into play.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an audience's response; he can pursue an absurd creation even when nobody asks to see it.

**Scenes**

- Friends hold a sheet and lamps while he invents the final figure in their shadow play.
  He can give his giant an entrance that surprises even the people making it possible.
- He has enough leftover dough to shape one impossible animal for the feast.
  He can give it seven heads and enjoy the making, whether anyone recognizes what he intended or not.

**Basis:** [Sun research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#v--creativity), challenged by [Firmicus's emphasis on friends' help](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#sun-in-v).
The collaborative pleasure is our adaptation of that outcome-oriented passage.

### Moon V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants an invented situation in which a feeling can run freely without explaining his life.
**Expressions:** He can relish theatrical misery; he can use play to approach a feeling he cannot yet name directly.

**Scenes**

- Children ask him to voice the rejected lover in their puppet play, then propose increasingly absurd misfortunes.
  He can prolong the delicious heartbreak or have the puppet fall extravagantly in love again.
- A storyteller invites each listener to change one thing in her tale of a traveler returning late.
  He can make the door open before the knock or leave the traveler outside, without telling anyone why.

**Basis:** [Moon research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Mercury V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the pleasure of inventing an effect and seeing what it makes possible.
**Expressions:** He can delight in a verbal game; he can spend extravagantly on a brief trick because he wants to see it work.

**Scenes**

- A flirt proposes a poem in which each line must contradict the previous one.
  He can prolong their exchange with an ingenious reversal instead of explaining what he really means.
- The remaining feast purse can hire two singers to deliver his riddle from opposite sides of the room.
  He can stage the absurd echo or try the device with his own voice, curious about the different effect.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#v--creativity) and the [historical resource-management comparison](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#mercury-in-v).
The spending motive is our invention; Firmicus's contrasting outcomes retain their phase condition in the notes.

### Venus V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to follow what delights him without first making it useful or lasting.
**Expressions:** He can lose himself in sensory play; he can enjoy being chosen by the person whose attention he wants.

**Scenes**

- Children ask him to costume the villain in their puppet play.
  He can revel in an extravagantly beautiful cloak or fit a hideous hat with bells that spoil every threatening entrance.
- Two festival guests invite him into a deliberately ridiculous dance.
  He can join for its absurdity or turn toward the one whose hand he has wanted to take all evening.

**Basis:** [Venus research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Mars V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the moment when desire becomes an attempt.
**Expressions:** He can seek playful risk for its own exhilaration; he can pursue the particular invitation or victory he wants.

**Scenes**

- A performer offers a turn on the low rope stretched over sawdust.
  He can step up for the thrill or demand another attempt after an undignified landing.
- Someone he desires waits beside the musicians while he invents reasons to approach.
  He can ask plainly for the next dance and enjoy having made the attempt before knowing the answer.

**Basis:** [Mars research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Jupiter V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants enjoyment to grow beyond the scale first imagined.
**Expressions:** He can revel in a lavish occasion; he can become attached to the pleasure of supplying more than anyone requested.

**Scenes**

- Children building a festival beast ask for one length of cloth.
  He can offer enough for a second creature or join in making their single beast magnificent.
- A storyteller offers him the final verse after the listeners have begun to rise.
  He can give the tale an extravagant new adventure or a splendid ending, enjoying the room's attention while it lasts.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Saturn V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to claim his limited time for a pleasure he takes seriously.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an exacting pastime for its own sake; he can hunger for play because too much time already feels lost, even while fearing foolishness.

**Scenes**

- An elderly dancer remembers a difficult step no one else requests anymore.
  The Prince can ask her to repeat it with him, enjoying the attempt without an audience or a finished performance.
- The fair is closing, and a puppeteer offers him one turn behind the little curtain.
  He can give the puppet an absurd voice or return it with the excuse that he is too old for this.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

## VI Labor

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vi--labor): necessary work, service, bodily care, and control over another person's effort.
The question is how a task is borne and under whose terms, rather than what its completion earns in public.
Limited authority over the work still permits choices about method, effort, or accepting care.

### Sun VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ordinary necessary work to bear something recognizably his.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy a competent result; he can want the person beside him to notice skill that disappears into daily provision.

**Scenes**

- His careful cutting leaves every loaf portion intact, and the kitchen empties the trays without remark.
  He can admire the last neat portion himself or ask the cook to look before serving it.
- His injured hand prevents lifting sacks, but seeds need sorting at a seated bench.
  He can take the task he still knows well or accept rest while admitting that being useful is what he misses.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Sun research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#vi--labor) and [Mars research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--sun-vi).

### Moon VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to feel needed in daily work without having to be available at every moment.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy knowing a routine's small needs; he can resent how readily others rely on that attention.

**Scenes**

  He can enjoy being the one it settles for or show the keeper the routine before moving to another task.
- At the ovens, everyone drinks when he brings the jug, but the overseer assigns another round during his break.
  He can pass the jug on or finish this round with his irritation plainly visible.

**Basis:** [Moon research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Mercury VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to turn understanding a task into a practical way of doing it.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an experiment that changes repetitive work; he can bargain over expertise others assume he will provide freely.

**Scenes**

- A laundress repeatedly stops to untangle the same pulley.
  He can fit a spare guide beside the rope and test it with the next load, interested in the mechanism as well as the relief.
- A workshop asks for a demonstration of his faster stitch before discussing his place at the bench.
  He can demonstrate or ask what access to that knowledge is worth to them.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Venus VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants affection to be felt in how necessary work is shared.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy giving or receiving practical care; he can want to be liked for more than his willingness to take another task.

**Scenes**

- A laundry worker warms water for his cracked hands before the next load.

**Basis:** [Venus research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Mars VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants command of how his assigned work gets finished.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy setting an effective pace; he can resent interference even when another method would also work.

**Scenes**

- At the packing bench, he is offered one crate to complete alone or a place in two workers' alternating rhythm.

**Basis:** [Mars research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Jupiter VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants work to leave room for a larger life, even when skill and goodwill secure no better terms.
**Expressions:** He can prefer variety to a dependable narrow task; he can seek fellowship and plenty within a day whose unfairness remains.

### Saturn VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the necessary work done on terms he can continue to bear.
**Expressions:** He can find satisfaction in reliable practice; he can remain committed while resenting the task or the person directing it.

**Scenes**

- A cooper needs the water casks readied before supper.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vi--labor).
Reed's enjoyment of service and Alice Sparkly Kat's dedication without enjoyment remain different possibilities.

### Venus VII — Relationships

### Mars VII — Relationships

### Jupiter VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a partnership that makes a larger undertaking imaginable.

**Scenes**

### Saturn VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a bond dependable enough that he need not keep proving himself a flawless partner.

**Scenes**

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).

## VIII Transformation

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#viii--transformation): death, bereavement, inheritance, and obligations attached to what passes between people.
Loss does not have to produce improvement or conceal a reward.
An ending can leave him choices about accepting, dividing, or relinquishing what remains.

### Sun VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to face how much of his independence rested on someone now gone.
**Expressions:** He can acknowledge support with affection; he can resist an account that makes his own choices seem borrowed.

**Scenes**

- An executor reveals that his late patron quietly paid the workshop rent during his first year of trade.
  He can receive the cancelled claim as a gift or repay it from his share of the estate, wanting to call that beginning his own.
- Sorting a dead mentor's papers, he finds a letter describing him as the continuation of her work.
  He can keep it among his own papers or leave it with her archive, still grateful for what she taught him.

**Basis:** [Sun research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Loss and dependence are our concrete setting for the source's concern with depth; neither source establishes these motives or estate arrangements.

### Moon VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants something to hold onto when a loss changes the support he relied on.
**Expressions:** He can seek reassurance from a surviving connection; he can prefer a concrete settlement to a conversation about grief.

**Scenes**

- After his companion's funeral, their common purse still pays for two places at the inn.
  He can take the refund or invite another mourner to share the last paid supper.
- An executor offers to discuss the dead guarantor's last days before settling the Prince's bond.
  He can ask to hear the account or put the payment on the table and attend only to the release.

**Basis:** [Moon research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Seeking and avoiding emotional intensity follow different readings; the loss and settlement are our house-based adaptation.

### Mercury VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants access to the knowledge that changes a settlement after loss.
**Expressions:** He can want an unfinished intention understood; he can use what others do not know to gain influence over the division.

**Scenes**

- A dead companion's instructions name an unfamiliar creditor.
  He can ask for the story behind the debt before distributing the belongings or settle it on the evidence already offered.
- Two heirs contest a boat, and he holds the receipt proving that a third owner paid for its refitting.
  He can produce it immediately or first seek a hearing for his own claim to the boat's equipment.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
The information has consequences for actual claims; inquiry need not make grief manageable or the Prince impartial.

### Venus VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a material settlement to recognize the particular value of an attachment.
**Expressions:** He can cherish a remnant without expecting it to heal him; he can want a share to acknowledge how much someone mattered to him.

**Scenes**

- An executor offers his late companion's cloak as part of his share.
  He can take it for the pleasure of its familiar weight or accept money while another mourner keeps it.
- A former partner proposes selling their jointly owned silver to settle the last shared debt.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Venus research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#viii--transformation) and [Moon research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--venus-viii).
Reciprocity, unreturned attachment, and material benefit are distinct source directions; the settlements are original combinations.

### Mars VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a decisive act where another person's claim limits what he can control.
**Expressions:** He can want a shared obligation settled at once; he can be angry that desiring an inheritance cannot make it wholly his.

**Scenes**

- A deceased partner's creditor will release their jointly owned boat for a stated settlement.
  He can pay immediately, surrender his share, or demand that the fittings be divided here and now.
- Two heirs must consent before a sealed chest is opened, and the other insists on a witness.
  He can fetch one briskly or press his demand to open it now, though his impatience does not remove their claim.

**Basis:** [Mars research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).

### Jupiter VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants room for a larger purpose in resources that a loss has placed partly in his hands.
**Expressions:** He can imagine a generous use for his share; he can think his own plan deserves more than the others agreed to give it.

**Scenes**

- A late friend's instruments are divided among heirs, and apprentices wait to learn which can still be used.
  He can offer his instrument to them or trade his other share for the collection he imagines keeping together.
- Co-heirs must divide the last common fund, and he wants a funeral feast large enough for everyone who knew the dead.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Imagining a use does not make him grateful for the death or establish that his purpose outweighs the other claims.

### Saturn VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to decide which claims survive a loss.
**Expressions:** He can accept a material loss to honor a chosen obligation; he can refuse a legacy that would give the dead continuing authority over his life.

**Scenes**

- An executor divides the last of an estate while someone the deceased supported waits outside the list of heirs.
  The Prince can include them in his portion or take the division as written, with affection and resentment still unsettled.
- A bequest is offered on condition that he sign the dead benefactor's account of their quarrel as true.
  He can sign for the inheritance or leave it unclaimed and keep his disagreement.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
The disputed claim and its settlement occur here; neither response creates a later tracked obligation or makes the death beneficial.

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ix--pilgrimage): distant journeys, unfamiliar practices, study, and beliefs tested beyond the familiar world.

### Sun IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a conviction he can affirm as his own beyond the world that first taught it to him.
**Expressions:** He can delight in wholehearted observance; he can decline to give his voice to a promise he does not share.

**Scenes**

- A distant shrine invites each visitor to add a verse naming what they hold sacred.

- A pilgrimage oath includes one promise he cannot endorse.
  He can ask to speak a different promise or visit without taking the oath, accepting that participation has several forms.

### Moon IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a distant place or practice to answer a longing the familiar world has not satisfied.
**Expressions:** He can delight in an experience without precedent; he can travel toward something that already feels half-remembered.

**Scenes**

- At a distant school, students exchange dream fragments before their morning observance.

**Basis:** [Moon research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage).

### Mercury IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants to think with an unfamiliar system well enough to question, translate, or carry it elsewhere.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy a distinction his teachers never supplied; he can want his explanation to influence others before his understanding is complete.

**Scenes**

- Fellow pilgrims ask him to explain a rite after his first lesson.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Mercury research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage) and [Jupiter research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--mercury-ix).

### Venus IX — Pilgrimage

**Scenes**

### Mars IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a belief or distant possibility to withstand an actual challenge.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy testing an unfamiliar practice; he can want to prevail in an argument that his hosts take seriously.

**Scenes**

### Jupiter IX — Pilgrimage

**Scenes**

- Friends planning an evening school debate whether its few seats should serve neighbors' children or visiting apprentices.
  He can argue for the company he imagined teaching or discover that their other purpose appeals to him.
- Old companions celebrate a failed expedition because they are glad they attempted it together.
  He can enjoy being one of them or admit, amid the welcome, that he does not want to join the next attempt.

**Basis:** [Sun research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).

### Moon XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a place in a circle whose shared hopes can change without leaving him outside.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy the modest welcome of acquaintances; he can miss an old common aim more than he expected.

**Scenes**

- A supper club remembers his favorite dish but little about his travels.
  He can enjoy being expected there or tell the table about the part of his life they have never met.
- Friends vote to turn their jointly planted garden into a meeting court.
  He can support the new plan or ask to keep one bed because tending it together was what brought him back.

**Basis:** [Moon research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).

### Mercury XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ideas to travel through people who will alter what they can become.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy an idea escaping its original circle; he can want to control the introduction that makes the next connection possible.

**Scenes**

- Friends sketch a night market, and he knows an instrument maker whose workshop could change their plan.
  He can invite her into the conversation and enjoy watching the proposal cease to be what they first imagined.
- Two circles ask him to bring the same ingenious acquaintance to their gatherings.
  He can introduce them all or choose one company, pleased that the connection presently depends on him.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).

### Venus XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the company and support of people whose particular qualities delight him.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy what friendship makes available; he can feel jealous when a favored friend's attention goes elsewhere.

**Scenes**

- Friends offer him a place in their evening music circle because they like his rough, funny songs.
  He can bring one eagerly or invite the friend whose company first made him want to join.
- His usual partner at a communal game has chosen a newcomer.
  He can enjoy watching their different styles, find a new partner, or admit he had looked forward to their familiar pairing.

**Basis:** [Venus research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).

### Mars XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants the pleasure of getting a shared intention underway.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy his friends' eager response; he can be jealous when someone else supplies the initiative.

**Scenes**

- Friends have talked for months about reopening their playing ground.
  He brings the tools, and they arrive ready to begin; he can enjoy being the person who got them moving.
- A companion announces the outing he meant to propose.
  He can join eagerly or argue for his own route, admitting that part of what he wanted was to start it himself.

**Basis:** [Mars research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#xi--friendship) and the [historical favorable comparison](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#mars-in-xi).
Being welcomed need not conceal a conflict or prove a causal claim about assertiveness.

### Jupiter XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants membership in a circle through which more becomes possible.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy accepting his friends' support; he can seek influence while also taking real pleasure in their company.

**Scenes**

- Friends have arranged the introduction he needs to join their expedition's planning meal.
  He can accept gladly and arrive with the questions he has wanted to ask for years.
- At a friend's gathering, the person whose patronage he wants is about to leave, just as the old company begins its favorite game.
  He can follow for an introduction or stay, without pretending only one invitation matters to him.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).

### Saturn XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants ties that can survive silence without continual proof of affection.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy being remembered by a few people; he can sustain a common effort while keeping his private life to himself.

**Scenes**

- Old companions have saved his place despite receiving no letters from him.
  He can sit down as though no explanation is owed or tell them why he stayed away.
- Friends ask him to sign a shared petition and tell the gathering the private experience behind his support.
  He can sign without telling the story, speak about it, or withdraw his name because the request has changed what joining means.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).
These people seek his participation as a member, not his authority as an officeholder.

## XII The Hidden

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xii--the-hidden): confinement, exclusion, retreat, and lives removed from ordinary access.
Chosen solitude and imposed isolation call for different encounters.
Limited access shapes what he can change, leaving room for relief without requiring escape or personal growth.

### Sun XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants a purpose he can inhabit when his usual identity is hidden or excluded.
**Expressions:** He can welcome anonymity; he can refuse to let confinement supply the whole account of his life.

**Scenes**

- A retreat asks guests to put away their insignia and remain unnamed until morning.
  He can enter with relief and choose how to spend an evening nobody will attribute to the Prince.
- In an exile hostel, a clerk asks him to list only the charge that bars his return.
  He can provide it or add the trade and work he still considers his, though the public gate remains closed.

**Basis:** [Sun research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Moon XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants contact or solitude on terms that let an unspoken feeling emerge.
**Expressions:** He can seek familiar company through a barrier; he can need privacy before knowing what he feels.

**Scenes**

- Confined behind the visitor's grille, he hears a friend's familiar market call from outside.
  He can ask for the day's ordinary news or let the friend sit nearby while neither speaks about the confinement.
- A retreat keeper gives him the inside bolt and promises not to call for supper.
  He can keep the door closed until his agitation finds a name or open it when familiar laughter reaches the corridor.

**Basis:** [Moon research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
The barrier and chosen retreat make access concrete; sadness alone does not establish this house.

### Mercury XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants control over how a thought travels when ordinary expression is restricted.
**Expressions:** He can find companionship through indirect language; he can keep an unfinished thought private rather than explain it on demand.

**Scenes**

- A confined correspondent sends a harmless-looking drawing through the laundry, carrying a joke only he recognizes.
  He can answer in the same form, enjoying a conversation the inspecting clerk does not share.
- During voluntary seclusion, a visitor asks to read the pages visible beneath his door.
  He can share one, turn them face down, or describe a different thought that leaves this one his.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Venus XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants affection or pleasure to have a place even when it cannot be publicly shared.
**Expressions:** He can cherish a private delight; he can want tenderness across an imposed restriction and resent how little contact is allowed.

**Scenes**

- A secluded retreat offers one room where a guest may sing unheard.
  He can take it gladly and sing the extravagant love songs he always shortens when someone is listening.
- A detained friend requests a familiar scent on the cloth allowed through the visiting grille.
  He can scent it as asked or ask them to keep the corner carrying his own perfume too.

**Basis:** [Venus research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
Private pleasure and restricted contact remain different possibilities; neither requires sacrifice to prove affection.

### Mars XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants room to act when scrutiny or exclusion constrains the ordinary route.
**Expressions:** He can enjoy working unseen; he can feel urgent anger when an institution controls whether his effort can reach someone.

**Scenes**

- In a retreat's closed workshop, he can finally attempt the mechanism he abandons whenever spectators offer advice.
  He can bolt the door and work or admit the one visitor he wants beside him.
- An exile barred from the city needs a reply before the gate closes, but the gatekeeper refuses to carry it.
  The Prince can deliver the permitted message outside himself or stop at the barrier, still furious at the obstruction.

**Basis:** [Mars research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Jupiter XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants hope for himself or a generous part in someone else's life when ordinary contact is cut off.
**Expressions:** He can sustain an observance without a promise of rescue; he can want to provide help and be remembered as its giver.

**Scenes**

- Confined to a closed lodging, he learns that the expected visitor with news cannot come today.
  He can keep the evening observance that has sustained him or let it lapse, disappointed without receiving an explanation for the delay.
- A relief keeper offers to carry his gift anonymously to detained travelers.
  He can send it unnamed or ask that they be told who remembered them, wanting both their relief and their gratitude.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden) and the [historical counterreading](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#jupiter-in-xii).
The scene permits hope; it does not reconcile modern protection claims with Valens's adverse judgment by guaranteeing an eventual reward.

### Saturn XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** The Prince wants some say over his separation from others.
**Expressions:** He can choose unobserved time without explaining or improving himself; he can anticipate judgment so thoroughly that an available contact feels foreclosed.

**Scenes**

- From a retreat room he hears the visitors gathering outside and rehearses every question they might ask.
  He can open the door, ask for one visitor, or leave it closed without the narration deciding whether this is rest or avoidance.
- Confined to a gatehouse, he is offered a supervised visit but must speak through the keeper.
  He can insist on delivering his own short message or refuse the visit rather than have the keeper speak for him.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
The confinement seed combines Houlding's house account with Saturnian restraint; it is an original synthesis, not a direct placement claim.

## Using the research

Choose a house situation, identify what this Prince wants through an occupying planet, and use the sign reference to shape his approach.
The entries are starting points for new scenes, not a requirement to repeat these props or offer the same two responses.
Compare neighboring entries when the planet or house could be swapped without changing the scene's reason for existing.
The research review records cases where a dynamic alone proved insufficient guidance.

Complete encounter menus, economic comparison, chart eligibility, and implementation remain subsequent work.
The research does not change the [economy table](../../client/src/data/economy.ts) or [narrative resolver](../../client/src/game/narrative.ts).
The next step is review of the full reference and its [findings](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_REVIEW.md) before selecting encounters to develop.

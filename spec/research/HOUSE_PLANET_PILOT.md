# House and planet research pilot

The eighteen-entry pilot for [issue #9](https://github.com/kronosapiens/SpacePrince/issues/9) is now incorporated into the [full 84-entry reference](../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md).
It researched Saturn across twelve houses and all seven planets in Achievement, with Saturn in Achievement researched independently in both assignments.
The original pilot is preserved in repository commit `b76199d`.

The [pilot review](HOUSE_PLANET_PILOT_REVIEW.md) retains the original comparisons, critique, scene exercise, and lessons for extending the method.
The [full research review](HOUSE_PLANET_REVIEW.md) records the continuation and assessment of all 84 entries.
The [Saturn notes](HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md) and [Achievement notes](HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md) retain the source evidence.

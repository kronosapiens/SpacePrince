# Research evidence and history

These records preserve sources, comparisons, and the reasoning behind earlier work.
Current design guidance lives in [concept](../concept/), [mechanics](../mechanics/), and [design](../design/).
An experiment or recommendation in a research record is not an accepted rule merely because the research is complete.
Active proposals belong in [drafts](../drafts/README.md).

## Source evidence

- The [house–planet source index](../concept/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#source-basis) links the original passages, qualifications, and research notes for all 84 placements.
- [Sign notes](SIGN_NOTES.md) record what each sign describes when it occupies a house — people, places, seasons, and manner — and how traditional sources combine a house with its sign.
- [FTL](FTL.md) and [Slay the Spire](STS.md) record encounter examples; [SURVEY.md](SURVEY.md) retains the analysis drawn from them.
- [Narrative voice](NARRATIVE_VOICE.md) retains source comparisons and measured opening lengths from selected game encounters, walking simulator developer accounts, and newspaper horoscope columns.
- [Achievement research](ACHIEVEMENTS.md), [FTL achievements](FTL_ACHIEVEMENTS.md), and [Slay the Spire achievements](STS_ACHIEVEMENTS.md) retain the corresponding comparisons.
- The [combat-entry Necessity research summary](https://github.com/kronosapiens/SpacePrince/pull/18#issuecomment-6457613560) records the balancing experiments and conclusions.
  The [evaluation methods guide](../../client/src/eval/README.md) describes future studies using the [current evaluator](../../client/README.md#balance-evaluation).

Keep source records needed by current citations, including disagreements and historical qualifications.
New accepted conclusions belong in the relevant current reference; historical records retain the context in which they were written.

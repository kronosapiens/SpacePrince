# House II scene briefs

These original situations follow [House II](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ii--livelihood): provisions and movable resources, including the pleasure of having enough, rather than a moral verdict on acquiring money.
The [sign notes](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply trades, seasons, and different habits of keeping or exchanging things.
Proposed exchanges concern what can be consumed, earned, or practiced here; no inventory, debt, or future delivery is required.
House II has no joy; each sign's five-scene pool currently includes two situations with an ordinary compulsory cost and three with voluntary trades or gifts.
Greater earning opportunities, deeper recovery, and useful capacity investments accompany that entry risk.
Every menu remains playable without a purchase, although the required exertion may be harmful or fatal.

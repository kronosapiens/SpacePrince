# House VI scene briefs

These original situations use [House VI](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vi--labor) for necessary work, illness, receiving care, small animals, and limited control over the conditions of effort.
[Mars VI](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#mars-vi--labor) informs an added choice that changes a method, tool, pace, or boundary and therefore contains a bodily burden.
The [sign notes](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply the work, season, and people's manner; they do not determine the Prince's temperament.
Economic directions may trade effort for immediate pay or gains, buy care, share a burden, or refuse it; no shift creates a continuing obligation.
Every finished menu has a valid way through at zero Light, which can be harmful or fatal.
Two scenes per sign begin with burdens already being borne, while the other scenes preserve voluntary work and ordinary care.
Greater work payments and stronger paid support accompany the added risk; Mars can limit or remove the burden through a changed method.

# House VII briefs

These candidates use [House VII](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vii--relationships) for agreements between people who can answer one another, and [SIGN_NOTES](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) for their circumstances and manner.
The [house–planet entries](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#vii-relationships) inform reciprocity without prescribing the Prince's motive.
Participation below names immediate agreements and ways of changing or ending them; this house has no joy.
One scene per sign begins after a journey, task, or shared expense is underway, so withdrawal can still carry a bodily or monetary cost.
The other encounters retain freely offered help and voluntary participation.

## Aries

1. `relationships-aries-first-oar` — The ferryman’s new partner pushes off while you are still finding your seat; spring haste, affectionate quarrel; take the strenuous second oar for the full crossing’s wage; pay the passenger fare and let her take the first turn; row back to the nearer landing and end the crossing there.
2. `relationships-aries-wedding-fire`: A bride and groom each brought a household coal, and neither will light the wedding brazier second; fire and direct pride, comedy; buy fresh tinder for both, hold the hot pan while they light it together, or let them settle it.
3. `relationships-aries-challenge`: Your old fencing partner offers a lesson with the padded sticks you once used, asking you to stop letting her win; frank rivalry, pleasure; practice hard for Afflict, take her stretching lesson for recovery, or sit out.
4. `relationships-aries-lambing`: A shepherd offers his dry cloak while you hold a newborn lamb, then admits it was his wife's cloak to lend; spring birth and unconsulted initiative, dilemma; pay for its cleaning, exchange places in the rain, or return it now.
5. `relationships-aries-apology`: The mason who shouted you off his site brings bread and an apology too loud for the quiet inn; blunt repair, generosity; accept the meal, share his paid roof-climb to inspect the finished work, or accept only his apology.

## Taurus

1. `relationships-taurus-bench` — The bench is halfway through your companion’s cottage window when the frame catches; fixed attachment and practical comfort, comedy; finish the heavy lift through the window for her offered payment; pay the porter to take your end through the window; lower your end carefully while she slides the bench back outside.
2. `relationships-taurus-last-slice`: A baker splits a rich cake for you and her husband, who quietly slides his larger piece toward you; bodily pleasure and stubborn kindness, gift; accept the restorative portion, divide it evenly, or leave it to him.
3. `relationships-taurus-draught`: A farmer proposes sharing the ploughing but refuses to lend the ox that knows his voice; familiar methods, negotiation; take the heavy handle for wages, pay him to finish your strip, or dissolve today's bargain.
4. `relationships-taurus-duet`: A singer invites you into a duet and refuses to change the low key that suits her; voice and steadiness, playful disagreement; practice the difficult part for Resolve, pay the accompanist for a comfortable key, or listen.
5. `relationships-taurus-ring`: A potter's partner wants their chipped wedding cups repaired, while the potter has laid out two new ones; durable affection and useful objects, dilemma; pay for repair and share tea, work the wheel for a lesson, or leave the choice with them.

## Gemini

1. `relationships-gemini-translation` — Halfway down the stairs, your interpreter explains that the merchant meant the other entrance; two versions of one agreement, comedy; carry the samples to the other entrance for their agreed fee; pay the porter to finish while you settle the wording with the interpreter; carry the case back onto the landing and end the arrangement.
2. `relationships-gemini-sides`: Two debaters ask you to take the opposite side halfway through their practice, but one has rehearsed only your objections; intellectual flexibility, pleasure; join a demanding full round for Fortune, take a short paid turn, or watch.
3. `relationships-gemini-window`: A neighbor speaks to you from the window where your partner used to leave messages, offering to read the old scratches aloud; familiar words with a new speaker, tenderness; sit for tea and recovery, pay for a careful tracing lesson, or leave the marks unread.
4. `relationships-gemini-double-booking`: A musician promised both you and another traveler the same quiet rehearsal hour; divided attention, irritation; share the hour for distributed recovery, buy the whole session for deeper rest, or reclaim the deposit.
5. `relationships-gemini-handshake`: A traveling conjurer offers to teach you the trick that emptied your purse, provided you return his handkerchief; nimble hands and candid bargaining, mischievous repair; accept a Fortune lesson, take your money back, or leave with the handkerchief returned.

## Cancer

1. `relationships-cancer-tide`: Your boat companion wants to wait for the tide at the tavern where her late husband always stopped; water and remembered habits, tenderness; share the meal, take her paid shift preparing the boat, or wait on the bank.
2. `relationships-cancer-room`: An innkeeper has saved you the room you once shared with a lover, unaware you have asked for separate beds; protective memory, awkward kindness; buy separate quiet rooms for shared recovery, keep the large room alone, or decline the lodging.
3. `relationships-cancer-recipe`: Your partner offers supper but asks you to stop guessing what her mother put in it; guarded family custom, comedy; enjoy the meal, help pound the unfamiliar spice for a lesson, or admit defeat and set the table.
4. `relationships-cancer-gangplank` — The boat shifts while you and its owner cross the gangplank with your bags; remembered reciprocity, dilemma; pay the dockhand to steady the crossing and take the bags; carry both heavy bags across for the fee the owner offers; divide the bags again and make the awkward crossing together.
5. `relationships-cancer-quiet-cup`: A brewer pours two cups after an argument and places yours just within reach without looking up; indirect reconciliation and familiar shelter, relief; accept a long restorative rest, share the small remaining pot, or take your leave gently.

## Leo

1. `relationships-leo-bow`: Your duet partner insists you take the last bow, though the audience is calling her name; generous pride, pleasure; accept the purse, split it for a shared supper, or insist she receive the applause.
2. `relationships-leo-shade`: A hunter offers the only shaded seat, then begins a story whose ending you know better than he does; summer heat and authority, comedy; rest and listen, buy drinks while correcting the story, or sit in the open for a paid account of your own.
3. `relationships-leo-banner` — Your rival’s banner is level with yours when the procession turns into a gust; visible equality, negotiation; hold the front position beside her for the procession fee; hire bearers and walk beside your rival without the weight; carry the poles into the side street and lower them together.
4. `relationships-leo-feast`: Your host has ordered an enormous supper for two and asks you not to tell anyone he cannot finish it; magnificence in private, affectionate embarrassment; eat at leisure, send portions to the waiting musicians for shared relief, or take only a cup.
5. `relationships-leo-rematch`: The woman who beat you at stone-throwing has kept both winning stones and offers you first choice; open rivalry and honest admiration, challenge; train with the heavier stone for Afflict, accept a small winner's gift, or congratulate her and leave.

## Virgo

2. `relationships-virgo-contract` — The carrier’s axle breaks after your bags have crossed the yard; exactness restrained by courtesy, negotiation; pay your agreed share for another cart to carry the bags out; share the heavy carrying and take her offered fee; carry your bags back through the narrow passage and cancel the rest.
3. `relationships-virgo-seeds`: Your gardening partner sorts two baskets of seed while you wait to plant, rejecting the handful you saved together; harvest discernment and shared ownership, quarrel; sort until dusk for wages, buy reliable seed, or plant nothing today.
4. `relationships-virgo-footnotes`: A scholar has written more corrections than congratulations in the letter announcing your arrival; devoted attention with an abrasive manner, comedy; take a concentrated lesson for Fortune, accept the offered lunch, or thank her for reading so closely.
5. `relationships-virgo-bandage`: A healer asks you to choose between her practiced hands and her apprentice's slower treatment, with the apprentice standing beside her; precision and trust, dilemma; purchase deep recovery, accept free modest care, or leave the dressing alone.

## Libra

1. `relationships-libra-uninvited-guest` — Supper is finished before your partner admits she invited your old opponent to reconcile you; forced harmony, comedy; pay your share and let the attempted reconciliation end here; wash your share of the supper dishes to settle the bill; work the full kitchen turn and collect the wages left after supper.
2. `relationships-libra-dance`: Your dancing partner asks you to lead the unfamiliar turn because she is tired of apologizing for knowing it better; courtesy and reciprocity, pleasure; learn the strenuous turn for Fortune, share an easy dance for recovery, or watch together.
3. `relationships-libra-seal`: A merchant wants the two of you to break a disputed seal together so neither can claim the other opened it; balanced accountability, negotiation; accept paid responsibility, buy an independent witness, or withdraw your claim.
4. `relationships-libra-two-gifts`: A former lover brings a gift exactly equal to the one you returned, insisting this makes the farewell simpler; measured exchange and unsettled affection, bittersweet comedy; accept its sale value, share a last restorative meal, or return it without matching anything.
5. `relationships-libra-veranda`: Your host offers a quiet veranda only if you will join a tiresome guest she cannot politely dismiss; social balance and hospitality, dilemma; take the cheap rest, pay for a separate room, or leave her to the guest.

## Scorpio

## Sagittarius

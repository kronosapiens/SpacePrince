# House XII briefs

[House XII](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xii--the-hidden) concerns lives outside ordinary access, including chosen solitude as well as imposed confinement.
[Sign references](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) make those lives particular; [Saturn in XII](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#saturn-xii--the-hidden) adds a workable limit or method within the circumstances.
No visit cures an exile or creates a later obligation; participation and its costs end in the scene.
A revealed confinement can require Light or affliction to bear, alongside scenes of wanted solitude and relief.
Larger paid returns and deeper quiet recovery accompany the more frequent burdens.

## Aries

1. `hidden-aries-charcoal`: The visitor is already inside the hot kiln ring; take the exposed exit, tend a watch, or finish the heavier closure for more pay; Saturn: limit the work to one shaded circuit.
2. `hidden-aries-new-hermit`: A new hermit has cleared a retreat so vigorously that he now has nowhere shaded to sit and asks you not to call it a mistake; beginnings and exposed haste, comedy; buy his spare mat or help lift a screen for pay; Saturn: set the screen at the one usable shaded corner.
3. `hidden-aries-cell-exercise`: A confined runner has worn a track into his cell and asks you to count laps through the door, paying for as long as your voice lasts; movement within limits, restless pleasure; count a brief or exhausting session; Saturn: set a finite interval and count it once.
4. `hidden-aries-red-door`: The delivery is already bearing on the Prince in a narrow alley; finish by the open or screened route, or lower it at the threshold; Saturn: use a support and smaller bundles.
5. `hidden-aries-unlit-forge`: A smith who closed his forge offers a quiet afternoon among the covered anvils; fire deliberately withheld, relief; accept deep rest without reopening his work, or leave him alone; Saturn: shut the street-facing shutters for deeper privacy and Resolve.

## Taurus

3. `hidden-taurus-muffled-song`: A singer rehearses behind thick curtains because she wants one song that no audience has claimed; voice and guarded pleasure, intimacy; buy a quiet listening hour or join a difficult low part; Saturn: agree on one verse and let the rest remain hers.

5. `hidden-taurus-hidden-supper`: Someone barred from a family celebration has ordered the same supper in a room next door and wants company that will not report back; appetite and excluded belonging, tenderness; purchase deep shared quiet or accept the small free portion; Saturn: close the connecting shutter before sitting.

## Cancer

3. `hidden-cancer-locked-cupboard`: A retired host lends you her own cup while leaving the visitors' set locked away and their knocks unanswered; hospitality withdrawn, quiet pleasure; take deep warmth, spend an afternoon gaining Resolve, or leave her the room; Saturn: wash your own cup and settle into shared recovery without opening the cupboard.
4. `hidden-cancer-island-room`: The sanctuary's causeway is already covered and its keeper has a warm room, an exposed landing, and a flooded storeroom; water and bounded retreat, shelter under constraint; pay for a night's rest that adds Resolve, bear the cold wait, or work the long pump shift for a large fee; Saturn: accept a limited tide watch in the narrow dry room.
5. `hidden-cancer-mother-silence`: A woman excluded from family visits pays to sort the envelopes filling a trunk she cannot lift; memory under protection, delicate work; take a short paid bundle, the more tiring and better-paid trunk, or return it unopened; Saturn: agree to broad date bundles and leave the open letters in her keeping.

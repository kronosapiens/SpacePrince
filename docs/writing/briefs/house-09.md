# House IX briefs

[House IX](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ix--pilgrimage) gives each encounter a foreign practice, subject of study, or observance whose authority belongs to someone present.
[Sign references](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) shape the practice and its people; the [Sun in IX](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#sun-ix--pilgrimage) supplies an additional way to participate with an understood conviction.
The Sun directions below are authored actions, not new lore or predictions.
Each pool retains ordinary help; an entered scene can require effort or payment even without the Sun's option.

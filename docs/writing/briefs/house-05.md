These original situations use [House V](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#v--creativity) for play, children, courtship, making, and pleasure that needs no useful product or public rank.
[Venus V](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#venus-v--creativity) supplies additional ways of attending to a wanted person, sensation, or creation; the [sign notes](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) supply varied practices and manners.
Participation may choose among pleasures, commitment sizes, payment by effort or Light, and individual or shared enjoyment without giving every gathering a paid job.
Any revival direction below welcomes someone back into the immediate occasion; the finished menu still fits five choices.
Most occasions remain freely offered, while one scene per sign begins with a bodily burden whose ordinary responses cost effort or Light.
Venus can make a harmless way through available.

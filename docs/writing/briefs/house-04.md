# House IV scene briefs

These original situations follow [House IV](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iv--home) and its [planetary expressions](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#iv-home): shelter, household memory, private authority, and a place that can be occupied differently now.
The [sign notes](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) shape each household's people, season, and manner without assigning a personality to the Prince.
The four accepted scenes retain their IDs and choices from [SCENES.md](../SCENES.md).
Every scene includes a paid revival as an immediate welcome or return; this fantasy does not require someone in the opening to have literally died.
House IV has no joy; each sign's five-scene pool includes one ordinary compulsory cost and four scenes retaining gifts or voluntary participation.
Crowded rooms, failed furnishings, and difficult sleeping conditions can require effort or payment after entry.
Every scene retains a valid zero-Light choice, which can be harmful, and its existing paid revival.

# House VIII briefs

[House VIII](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#viii--transformation) supplies concrete endings, inheritances, and resources controlled by others; [SIGN_NOTES](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md) varies how those affairs occur.
These are original situations, not claims that bereavement produces growth.
Payments settle here and objects are used or relinquished here; no joy belongs to this house.
Two scenes per sign begin with an existing burden, journey, or immediate bill, so every ordinary response has a cost before favorable occupant terms.
Other scenes preserve freely offered care and settlements, while the larger paid tasks and lessons supply greater opportunities.
Every menu remains resolvable at zero Light, even when its consequence is harmful or fatal.

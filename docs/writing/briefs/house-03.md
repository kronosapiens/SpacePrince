# House III scene briefs

These original situations use [House III](../HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iii--communication) for siblings, neighbors, familiar routes, and ordinary messages whose reception matters now.
[Moon III](../HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#moon-iii--communication) supplies additional approaches through shared habits, remembered details, and practical reassurance; [sign references](../../research/SIGN_NOTES.md#the-twelve-signs) shape the people and the exchange.
Economic directions remain provisional and should follow the chosen participation, without adding a transaction merely to fill a menu.
In particular, answering a relative or receiving a welcome does not need an unrelated delivery fee or a new labor task.
Each sign's five-scene pool includes one ordinary compulsory cost on a familiar route or exchange, alongside four scenes of help or voluntary participation.
Moon's available approach often removes that burden, though a shared heavy load can still require reduced exertion.
Every scene has a valid zero-Light choice, which may be harmful.

# House and planet matrix

A researched authoring reference for all 84 combinations of seven planets and twelve houses in Space Prince.
Each entry gives the Prince a central want, two possible expressions, and a link to the source reasoning.
The [house reference](HOUSE_MATRIX.md) supplies situations and their structural character; the [planet and sign reference](PLANET_MATRIX.md) supplies planetary domains and ways of expressing them.

Houses: [I Self](#i-self) · [II Livelihood](#ii-livelihood) · [III Communication](#iii-communication) · [IV Home](#iv-home) · [V Creativity](#v-creativity) · [VI Labor](#vi-labor) · [VII Relationships](#vii-relationships) · [VIII Transformation](#viii-transformation) · [IX Pilgrimage](#ix-pilgrimage) · [X Achievement](#x-achievement) · [XI Friendship](#xi-friendship) · [XII The Hidden](#xii-the-hidden).

## Reading the entries

The house supplies the situation; the occupying planet supplies a concern or form of agency; the sign shapes its expression.
This matrix is indexed by **House + Planet**; the Prince's natal chart determines which of its 84 pairings are occupied.
Each house introduction includes its [fixed House attributes](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#house-structure), such as angularity and favorability, once for all seven entries.
The occupant's Sign supplies expression through the separate Planet–Sign reference, and that pairing also determines dignity.
The [encounter lookup model](../mechanics/HOUSES.md#3-chart-conditioning-model) and [composition guidance](../mechanics/ENCOUNTERS.md#46-composing-and-revising-a-scenario) describe how these references contribute to options and economic terms.
The Prince takes the action and can want pleasure, company, power, possession, recognition, relief, or an answer without becoming an example of good conduct.
The [drives guidance](DRIVES.md) distinguishes the Prince's motives from the player's experience.
Its vocabulary remains provisional; the entries do not allocate each planet a single drive or require every scene to end in growth.

[You are the Prince](../intent.md#the-prince), so the entries speak in the second person: the Prince is you in the fiction, and the player is you at the table.
**Dynamic** states what matters to the Prince here.
**Expressions** offer different possibilities, not fixed personalities or opposite moral outcomes.
Both are original game adaptations.
A scene can end with uncomplicated enjoyment, an unresolved feeling, or a refusal.

Occupancy refers to the Prince's own chart, separately from the house's fixed ruler and planetary joy.
The fixed ruler supplies the encounter's color and musical identity; a joy is a separate affinity.
Under whole-sign houses, planets sharing a house also share its sign.

## Source basis

Research completed on 2026-10-04 includes direct contemporary placement readings for every entry and selected historical comparisons.
The notes identify authors, passage locators, conditions, disagreements, and inaccessible material.
A historical prediction supplies neither a psychological motive nor a promise of a game outcome.
These readings are literary reference material, not evidence that chart placements cause personalities.

## I Self

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#i--self): presence, bodily life, and how a person introduces themselves.
The encounter concerns inhabiting an identity before it becomes a public reputation.
Your own gesture can establish how the encounter proceeds.

### Sun I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want to recognize yourself in the person you present.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy taking up space; you can want an altered appearance accepted without accounting for the change.

**Basis:** [Sun research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Moon I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want room for what your body reveals before you have chosen an introduction.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy arriving visibly delighted; you can resent being asked to explain a sadness already audible in your voice.

**Basis:** [Moon research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Mercury I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want to discover how you can present yourself through a quick response.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy an improvised introduction; you can resent being so familiar that somebody else supplies your answer.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Venus I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want pleasure in your own presence, including the way it attracts another person's attention.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy being appealing; you can tire of the agreeable manner through which people first welcomed you.

**Basis:** [Venus research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Mars I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want to meet the situation through an act of your own before a role is assigned to you.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy an immediate bodily attempt; you can become impatient with even a flattering introduction that delays participation.

**Basis:** [Mars research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Jupiter I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want to arrive as someone for whom more is possible than your present circumstances suggest.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy expansive confidence; you can enjoy the expectations your entrance raises before earning them.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#i--self).

### Saturn I — Self

**Dynamic:** You want possession of your own bearing before another person decides how you should appear.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy composure; you can also resent the effort of maintaining it and want someone else to take care of you.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, I](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#i--self), especially Alice Sparkly Kat's discussion of control and composure.

## II Livelihood

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ii--livelihood): usable possessions, income, provisions, and material sufficiency.
Resources here sustain the living traveler; an inheritance belongs to the Eighth and a shared undertaking to the Eleventh.
An offer can leave choices about acquisition or retention even when resources are abundant.

### Sun II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want your possessions to give substance to your sense of capability and worth.
**Expressions:** You can take pride in what you have earned; you can feel diminished by a perfectly useful possession that fails to represent you.

**Basis:** [Sun research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Moon II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want your provisions to make you feel supplied, as well as keep you supplied.
**Expressions:** You can savor a full bag; you can spend on immediate comfort when another prudent purchase would leave the day just as bleak.

**Basis:** [Moon research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Mercury II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want your understanding of things to improve what you can obtain from an exchange.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy finding an overlooked use; you can insist on time to appraise something before accepting another person's valuation.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Venus II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want your resources to furnish a life you actually enjoy.
**Expressions:** You can spend on a wanted pleasure; you can give generously while hoping your taste or contribution will be appreciated.

**Basis:** [Venus research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Mars II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want the power to acquire and use what you want on your own terms.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy securing the means of your next undertaking; you can guard a possession simply because it is yours.

**Basis:** [Mars research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Jupiter II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want provision ample enough to widen the life you can choose.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy having more than today's necessities; you can want abundance because modest sufficiency feels too small for your plans.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).

### Saturn II — Livelihood

**Dynamic:** You want provision you can rely on without feeling owned by its provider.
**Expressions:** You can take satisfaction in making your supplies last; you can suspect dependence even when help comes freely.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, II](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#ii--livelihood).
Suspected dependence is the Prince's concern, not a concealed debt.

## III Communication

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iii--communication): neighbors, siblings, everyday messages, and familiar short journeys.
An ordinary exchange is the immediate stake, even when it carries affection or responsibility.
Practical help can be close at hand while the exchange depends on another person's response.

### Sun III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want your own account to matter among the people who know you.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy being sought for an explanation; you can resent a familiar person's power to puncture your version of events.

**Basis:** [Sun research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Moon III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want ordinary words and shared habits to carry a feeling of connection.
**Expressions:** You can delight in a shared verbal habit; you can shape ordinary news to invite reassurance or keep someone from worrying.

**Basis:** [Moon research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Mercury III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want the next detail or discovery moving through the world you know.
**Expressions:** You can relish news without needing to settle it; you can test a familiar explanation that no longer accounts for what you notice.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#iii--communication) and [Firmicus's discovery passage](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#mercury-in-iii).

### Venus III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want ordinary words to make you someone a familiar listener is pleased to hear from.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy making a familiar person laugh; you can flatter because you want their attention to return to you.

**Basis:** [Venus research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Mars III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want to answer familiar people directly, with enough force to change the exchange.
**Expressions:** You can relish an argument both parties enjoy; you can insist on confronting the person behind a circulating complaint.

**Basis:** [Mars research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#iii--communication).

### Jupiter III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want familiar conversation to open a wider prospect, and like being the person who offers it.
**Expressions:** You can delight in connecting neighbors' possibilities; you can give more advice than someone sought because being consulted pleases you.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#iii--communication).
Enjoying influence is an original extension of the source's advisory role.

### Saturn III — Communication

**Dynamic:** You want to speak among familiar people without every word having to serve as an example.
**Expressions:** You can lend weight to a carefully considered message; you can use silence or dry humor to escape being the dependable authority.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, III](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iii--communication).
The authority comes from repeated local contact, rather than a public office.

## IV Home

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#iv--home): shelter, roots, household memory, land, and private endings.
Care here concerns a place and its history, rather than every instance of comfort or kindness.
Private authority matters: establish who can offer shelter and who sets its immediate terms.

### Sun IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want your home to express the life you regard as your own.
**Expressions:** You can take pride in an inherited foundation; you can give a household a form that your relatives would not have chosen.

**Basis:** [Sun research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Moon IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want a dwelling whose ordinary habits answer your present need to belong.
**Expressions:** You can welcome being cared for without explanation; you can want a different room even in a household you love.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Moon research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#iv--home) and [Venus research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--moon-iv).

### Mercury IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want to think through a household's stories and possibilities with the people who live there.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy thinking aloud among familiar people; you can want to understand a family object whose history no longer seems settled.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Venus IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want your dwelling to give tangible room to the people and pleasures you cherish.
**Expressions:** You can delight in preparing a particular welcome; you can become possessive about who occupies a cherished domestic place.

**Basis:** [Venus research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Mars IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want an active say over the space in which you live.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy changing a room with your own hands; you can want to prevail over another resident's equally ordinary preference.

**Basis:** [Mars research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Jupiter IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want a household spacious enough for the way of living you believe in.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy enlarging its welcome; you can want its customs to embody your ideals even when another resident disagrees.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#iv--home).

### Saturn IV — Home

**Dynamic:** You want continuity with a household whose familiar habits can also confine you.
**Expressions:** You can cherish an old arrangement without calling it happy; you can change one domestic habit without repudiating the whole home.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, IV](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#iv--home).
These are small acts within an inherited way of living, not instant reconciliation.

## V Creativity

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#v--creativity): play, pleasure, children, courtship, and making something for its own sake.
The activity can matter without producing useful goods or winning public recognition.
You can sustain an occasion, join in, or enjoy what others have made.

### Sun V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want the delight of bringing something unmistakably yours into play.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy an audience's response; you can pursue an absurd creation even when nobody asks to see it.

**Basis:** [Sun research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#v--creativity), challenged by [Firmicus's emphasis on friends' help](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#sun-in-v).

### Moon V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want an invented situation in which a feeling can run freely without explaining your life.
**Expressions:** You can relish theatrical misery; you can use play to approach a feeling you cannot yet name directly.

**Basis:** [Moon research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Mercury V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want the pleasure of inventing an effect and seeing what it makes possible.
**Expressions:** You can delight in a verbal game; you can spend extravagantly on a brief trick because you want to see it work.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#v--creativity) and the [historical resource-management comparison](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#mercury-in-v).
The spending motive is our invention; Firmicus's contrasting outcomes retain their phase condition in the notes.

### Venus V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want to follow what delights you without first making it useful or lasting.
**Expressions:** You can lose yourself in sensory play; you can enjoy being chosen by the person whose attention you want.

**Basis:** [Venus research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Mars V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want the moment when desire becomes an attempt.
**Expressions:** You can seek playful risk for its own exhilaration; you can pursue the particular invitation or victory you want.

**Basis:** [Mars research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Jupiter V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want enjoyment to grow beyond the scale first imagined.
**Expressions:** You can revel in a lavish occasion; you can become attached to the pleasure of supplying more than anyone requested.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#v--creativity).

### Saturn V — Creativity

**Dynamic:** You want to claim your limited time for a pleasure you take seriously.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy an exacting pastime for its own sake; you can hunger for play because too much time already feels lost, even while fearing foolishness.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, V](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#v--creativity).
Skill, embarrassment, and appetite belong to the pleasure itself; neither expression needs a useful product.

## VI Labor

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vi--labor): necessary work, service, bodily care, and control over another person's effort.
The question is how a task is borne and under whose terms, rather than what its completion earns in public.
Limited authority over the work still permits choices about method, effort, or accepting care.

### Sun VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want ordinary necessary work to bear something recognizably yours.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy a competent result; you can want the person beside you to notice skill that disappears into daily provision.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Sun research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#vi--labor) and [Mars research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--sun-vi).

### Moon VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want to feel needed in daily work without having to be available at every moment.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy knowing a routine's small needs; you can resent how readily others rely on that attention.

**Basis:** [Moon research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Mercury VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want to turn understanding a task into a practical way of doing it.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy an experiment that changes repetitive work; you can bargain over expertise others assume you will provide freely.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Venus VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want affection to be felt in how necessary work is shared.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy giving or receiving practical care; you can want to be liked for more than your willingness to take another task.

**Basis:** [Venus research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Mars VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want command of how your assigned work gets finished.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy setting an effective pace; you can resent interference even when another method would also work.

**Basis:** [Mars research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#vi--labor).

### Jupiter VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want work to leave room for a larger life, even when skill and goodwill secure no better terms.
**Expressions:** You can prefer variety to a dependable narrow task; you can seek fellowship and plenty within a day whose unfairness remains.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Jupiter research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#vi--labor) and [Mercury research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--jupiter-vi), including the [historical adverse judgment](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#jupiter-in-vi).
Variety follows Rough's different emphasis; neither generosity nor competence purchases good fortune.

### Saturn VI — Labor

**Dynamic:** You want the necessary work done on terms you can continue to bear.
**Expressions:** You can find satisfaction in reliable practice; you can remain committed while resenting the task or the person directing it.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vi--labor).
Reed's enjoyment of service and Alice Sparkly Kat's dedication without enjoyment remain different possibilities.

## VII Relationships

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#vii--relationships): a particular other person, partnership, agreements, and open disagreement.
Both parties have a say in the terms; a wider circle of support belongs to the Eleventh.
The parties' decisions can directly change what each gives, receives, or agrees to do.

### Sun VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want a partnership through which you can recognize the person you mean to become.
**Expressions:** You can feel proud to join someone you admire; you can outgrow a valued arrangement without ceasing to value the other person.

**Basis:** [Sun research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).
Accommodation, dominance, and equal partnership remain differing source emphases.

### Moon VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want this particular companion to recognize what makes the partnership feel secure to you.
**Expressions:** You can wish to be understood without asking; you can welcome a direct request that ends the guessing.

**Basis:** [Moon research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).

### Mercury VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want this particular counterpart to make thinking together worth the trouble of agreement.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy having your position changed; you can prolong an argument for the pleasure of winning the other's answer.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).

### Venus VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want the terms of being together to preserve what you enjoy about this person.
**Expressions:** You can delight in an arrangement made for two; you can be disappointed when your apparently perfect offer is declined.

**Basis:** [Venus research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).

### Mars VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want another person's force to sharpen or advance your own desire.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy being challenged by an equal; you can want a forceful ally to conduct a confrontation you would rather avoid.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Mars research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#vii--relationships) and [Sun research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--mars-vii).
Welcoming opposition and recruiting another's force are different readings, not stages of learning assertiveness.

### Jupiter VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want a partnership that makes a larger undertaking imaginable.
**Expressions:** You can welcome another person's confidence; you can expect your exciting proposal to deserve their agreement.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).

### Saturn VII — Relationships

**Dynamic:** You want a bond dependable enough that you need not keep proving yourself a flawless partner.
**Expressions:** You can take pleasure in a particular person's reliability; you can protect the agreement so closely that ordinary ease together starts to feel like another obligation.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#vii--relationships).
The other person's stated terms matter; this is not a test of whether the Prince learns to trust everyone.

## VIII Transformation

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#viii--transformation): death, bereavement, inheritance, and obligations attached to what passes between people.
Loss does not have to produce improvement or conceal a reward.
An ending can leave you choices about accepting, dividing, or relinquishing what remains.

### Sun VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want to face how much of your independence rested on someone now gone.
**Expressions:** You can acknowledge support with affection; you can resist an account that makes your own choices seem borrowed.

**Basis:** [Sun research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Loss and dependence are our concrete setting for the source's concern with depth; neither source establishes these motives or estate arrangements.

### Moon VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want something to hold onto when a loss changes the support you relied on.
**Expressions:** You can seek reassurance from a surviving connection; you can prefer a concrete settlement to a conversation about grief.

**Basis:** [Moon research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Seeking and avoiding emotional intensity follow different readings; the loss and settlement are our house-based adaptation.

### Mercury VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want access to the knowledge that changes a settlement after loss.
**Expressions:** You can want an unfinished intention understood; you can use what others do not know to gain influence over the division.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
The information has consequences for actual claims; inquiry need not make grief manageable or the Prince impartial.

### Venus VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want a material settlement to recognize the particular value of an attachment.
**Expressions:** You can cherish a remnant without expecting it to heal you; you can want a share to acknowledge how much someone mattered to you.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Venus research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#viii--transformation) and [Moon research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--venus-viii).
Reciprocity, unreturned attachment, and material benefit are distinct source directions; the settlements are original combinations.

### Mars VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want a decisive act where another person's claim limits what you can control.
**Expressions:** You can want a shared obligation settled at once; you can be angry that desiring an inheritance cannot make it wholly yours.

**Basis:** [Mars research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).

### Jupiter VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want room for a larger purpose in resources that a loss has placed partly in your hands.
**Expressions:** You can imagine a generous use for your share; you can think your own plan deserves more than the others agreed to give it.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
Imagining a use does not make you grateful for the death or establish that your purpose outweighs the other claims.

### Saturn VIII — Transformation

**Dynamic:** You want to decide which claims survive a loss.
**Expressions:** You can accept a material loss to honor a chosen obligation; you can refuse a legacy that would give the dead continuing authority over your life.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, VIII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#viii--transformation).
The disputed claim and its settlement occur here; neither response creates a later tracked obligation or makes the death beneficial.

## IX Pilgrimage

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#ix--pilgrimage): distant journeys, unfamiliar practices, study, and beliefs tested beyond the familiar world.
Give the learning a subject and the observance an action.
Guidance can be generous even where the Prince has little authority over the setting.

### Sun IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** You want a conviction you can affirm as your own beyond the world that first taught it to you.
**Expressions:** You can delight in wholehearted observance; you can decline to give your voice to a promise you do not share.

**Basis:** [Sun research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage) and the [historical hymn-making comparison](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#sun-in-ix).
Sincere affirmation is our chosen motive, not an inference from predicted fame.

### Moon IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** You want a distant place or practice to answer a longing the familiar world has not satisfied.
**Expressions:** You can delight in an experience without precedent; you can travel toward something that already feels half-remembered.

**Basis:** [Moon research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage).
Novelty and return remain alternative readings, not proof that the unfamiliar place is your true home.

### Mercury IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** You want to think with an unfamiliar system well enough to question, translate, or carry it elsewhere.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy a distinction your teachers never supplied; you can want your explanation to influence others before your understanding is complete.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Mercury research, IX](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#ix--pilgrimage) and [Jupiter research overlap](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#independent-overlap--mercury-ix).

### Venus IX — Pilgrimage

**Dynamic:** You want to approach a distant way of living through what first attracts you to it.
**Expressions:** You can let a beautiful form invite participation; you can want company whose life cannot be fitted into your existing plans.

### Mars IX — Pilgrimage

### Jupiter IX — Pilgrimage

## X Achievement

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#x--achievement): public standing, authority, credit, and responsibility for visible work.
The presence of an audience changes what is at stake.
A decision can carry public force without receiving an automatic economic advantage.

### Sun X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want public action to carry your authorship, including the authority to say what it means.
**Expressions:** You can take pleasure in an honor as your own; you can refuse a prestigious role that makes you another person's emblem.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Sun](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#sun-in-achievement).

### Moon X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** Public success matters to you through the people and memories that make it feel like acceptance.
**Expressions:** You can savor being welcomed back; you can resent the familiar role an audience still needs you to play.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Moon](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#moon-in-achievement).
The public occasion exposes a private attachment; it does not require the crowd to be wrong about you.

### Mercury X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want a voice that can change what publicly counts as an achievement.
**Expressions:** You can relish a precise distinction that changes an official account; you can resist being confined to the one expertise for which you are already known.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Mercury](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#mercury-in-achievement).
These follow different source emphases: command of a formal account and appetite for changing work.

### Venus X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want distinction to be delightful to inhabit.
**Expressions:** You can savor admiration without asking for intimacy; you can want others to love what delights you, using your standing to shape a shared occasion.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Venus](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#venus-in-achievement).
Shaping a shared occasion through an admired reputation is our development; charm does not establish dishonesty.

### Mars X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want the authority to attempt something decisive in your own way.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy prevailing against a rival; you can refuse an honor that leaves you unable to act.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Mars](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#mars-in-achievement).
Competitive appetite and command give these expressions their force without requiring bodily strain or a righteous opponent.

### Jupiter X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want a public position large enough for what you imagine doing through it.
**Expressions:** You can accept an opening with delighted confidence; you can relish granting recognition and making your vision the measure of what deserves support.

**Basis:** [Achievement research, Jupiter](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#jupiter-in-achievement).
Making patronage serve the Prince's own vision is an original extension, with thinner direct support than the source themes of advancement and worthwhile work.

### Saturn X — Achievement

**Dynamic:** You want an earned public place to endure without having to justify your presence forever.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy authority that is finally trusted; you can cling to the undertaking and its people because handing it on feels like erasure.

**Basis:** Independently developed [Saturn research, X](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#x--achievement) and [Achievement research, Saturn](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md#saturn-in-achievement).
Public charge gives these attachments their setting; company alone would belong to Friendship.

## XI Friendship

Assistance can be freely given without creating an unrecorded obligation.
Support gives you means to take part in a present undertaking, whether you feel welcome, grateful, envious, or uneasy.

### Sun XI — Friendship

### Moon XI — Friendship

### Mercury XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** You want ideas to travel through people who will alter what they can become.

### Venus XI — Friendship

**Dynamic:** You want the company and support of people whose particular qualities delight you.

### Mars XI — Friendship

### Jupiter XI — Friendship

### Saturn XI — Friendship

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XI](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xi--friendship).
These people seek your participation as a member, not your authority as an officeholder.

## XII The Hidden

[House basis](HOUSE_MATRIX.md#xii--the-hidden): confinement, exclusion, retreat, and lives removed from ordinary access.
Chosen solitude and imposed isolation call for different encounters.
Limited access shapes what the Prince can change, leaving room for relief without requiring escape or personal growth.

### Sun XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want a purpose you can inhabit when your usual identity is hidden or excluded.
**Expressions:** You can welcome anonymity; you can refuse to let confinement supply the whole account of your life.

**Basis:** [Sun research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SUN_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Moon XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want contact or solitude on terms that let an unspoken feeling emerge.
**Expressions:** You can seek familiar company through a barrier; you can need privacy before knowing what you feel.

**Basis:** [Moon research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MOON_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
The barrier and chosen retreat make access concrete; sadness alone does not establish this house.

### Mercury XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want control over how a thought travels when ordinary expression is restricted.
**Expressions:** You can find companionship through indirect language; you can keep an unfinished thought private rather than explain it on demand.

**Basis:** [Mercury research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MERCURY_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Venus XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want affection or pleasure to have a place even when it cannot be publicly shared.
**Expressions:** You can cherish a private delight; you can want tenderness across an imposed restriction and resent how little contact is allowed.

**Basis:** [Venus research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_VENUS_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
Private pleasure and restricted contact remain different possibilities; neither requires sacrifice to prove affection.

### Mars XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want room to act when scrutiny or exclusion constrains the ordinary route.
**Expressions:** You can enjoy working unseen; you can feel urgent anger when an institution controls whether your effort can reach someone.

**Basis:** [Mars research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_MARS_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).

### Jupiter XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want hope for yourself or a generous part in someone else's life when ordinary contact is cut off.
**Expressions:** You can sustain an observance without a promise of rescue; you can want to provide help and be remembered as its giver.

**Basis:** [Jupiter research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_JUPITER_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden) and the [historical counterreading](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md#jupiter-in-xii).
The observance permits hope; it does not reconcile modern protection claims with Valens's adverse judgment by guaranteeing an eventual reward.

### Saturn XII — The Hidden

**Dynamic:** You want some say over your separation from others.
**Expressions:** You can choose unobserved time without explaining or improving yourself; you can anticipate judgment so thoroughly that an available contact feels foreclosed.

**Basis:** [Saturn research, XII](../research/HOUSE_PLANET_SATURN_NOTES.md#xii--the-hidden).
This dynamic combines Houlding's house account with Saturnian restraint; it is an original synthesis, not a direct placement claim.

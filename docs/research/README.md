# Research: evidence and history

These records preserve sources, comparisons, and the reasoning behind earlier work.
Current guidance lives in [intent](../intent.md), [mechanics](../mechanics/), [presentation](../presentation/), and [writing](../writing/); an experiment or recommendation recorded here is not an accepted rule because the research is complete.
Proposals and open questions live in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/kronosapiens/SpacePrince/issues).
Observations keep the dates and qualifications they were written with; update the navigation here rather than rewriting old findings as current results.

## Astrology, houses, and signs

- [ASTROLOGY.md](ASTROLOGY.md): a historically grounded guide to astrology as a symbolic system, era by era, with the sources behind favorable and unfavorable houses, dignities, and sect.
- [Sign notes](SIGN_NOTES.md): what each sign describes when it occupies a house (people, places, seasons, manner), and how traditional sources combine a house with its sign.
- The house–planet research notes, one per planet plus the [traditional](HOUSE_PLANET_TRADITIONAL_NOTES.md) and [achievement](HOUSE_PLANET_ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES.md) notes, are indexed from the [source basis](../writing/HOUSE_PLANET_MATRIX.md#source-basis) of the house and planet matrix, which links the original passages and qualifications for all 84 placements.

## Games

- [FTL](FTL.md) and [Slay the Spire](STS.md) inventory the two games' text events; [SURVEY.md](SURVEY.md) is the analysis drawn from them, written against the narrative encounters as they stood in September 2026.
- [Narrative voice](NARRATIVE_VOICE.md) compares opening lengths and voice across selected game encounters, walking-simulator developer accounts, and newspaper horoscope columns.
- [Achievement research](ACHIEVEMENTS.md), with the [FTL](FTL_ACHIEVEMENTS.md) and [Slay the Spire](STS_ACHIEVEMENTS.md) achievement inventories, compares the two catalogues and proposes twenty candidates; the client draws twelve of them as reserved marks on the Prince artwork, with no earning logic yet.
